the passage of time, I started wearing ‘Imamah as well. Upon this, some of my family members strictly opposed me; even sometime they would remove my ‘Imamah by pulling it. Despite all these difficulties, the attraction of Madani environment and politeness of devotees of Rasool were bringing me closer and closer to Dawat-e-Islami. The Ijtima’ of Islamic sisters started at home. My father grew a beard. At the time of writing, I have had the responsibility of a ‘Majlis’ of Dawat-e-Islami, a non-political global movement of preaching Quran and Sunnah.
