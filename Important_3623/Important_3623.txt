Sharih-e-Bukhari Hazrat Imam Qastalani رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه farmatay hayn: Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ us shakhs par rahmat naazil farmaye jis nay maah-e-wiladat ki rato ko eid bana liya. (Mawahib ul Ladunya, jild. 1, pp. 148)

Eid-e-Meelad-ul-Nabi to eid ki bhi eid hay

Bil yaqeen hay eid-e-eidan eid Melaad-ul-Nabi

Agar Huzoor صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم ki is na paydar dunya may jalwah gari na hoti to yaqeenan koi eid, eid hoti aur na hi koi shab, shab-e-qadr ya shab-e-bara`at hoti. Ye Eidayn aur mubarak ratayan hamayn aap صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم hi ka sadaqay mili hayn. Lihaza hamayn chahiye kay hum aashiqon ki is eid par aik Azeem na’mat milnay ki khushi may Jashn-e-wiladat ki is qadar dhoomayn machaayen kay shaytan la’een dum daba ker bhaag jaye. اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰہ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ na’mat milnay par khushi ka izhar kerna aur is din eid manana to aysa mubarak amal hay kay jis ka suboot hamayn Quran-e-Kareem say bhi milta hay chunanchay

Parah 7 Surah Mai`dah ki aayat number 114 may Irshad hota hay:

قَالَ عِیْسَى ابْنُ مَرْیَمَ اللّٰهُمَّ رَبَّنَاۤ اَنْزِلْ عَلَیْنَا مَآىٕدَةً مِّنَ السَّمَآءِ تَكُوْنُ لَنَا عِیْدًا لِّاَوَّلِنَا وَ اٰخِرِنَا وَ اٰیَةً مِّنْكَۚ-

Tarjama kanz ul iman: E’sa bin Maryam nay ‘arz ki aye Allah Aye Rab hamaray hum par aasman say aik khuwan utar kay woh hamaray liye eid ho hamaray aglay picchlo ki aur tayri taraf say nishani. (Parah. 7, Sorah. Maaidah, Aayat. 114)

Bayan kardah aayat e Muqaddasah kay tahat Tafseer Sirat-ul-Jinan may hay is aayat say ma’loom huwa kay jis roz Allah ta’aala ki khas rahmat naazil ho us din ko eid banana, khushiyan manana ibadatayn kerna aur Khuda ka shukr baja lana Saliheen ka Tareeqah hay aur bay shak Sarkar صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم ki tashreef aawari yaqeenan Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki Azeem tareen na’mat aur buzurg tareen rahmat hay is liye Huzoor صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم ki wiladat kay din eid manana aur Meelad Shareef parh ker khuda ka shukar baja lana aur farhat o suroor ka izhar kerna qabil-e-ta’reef aur Allah kay maqbool bando ka tareqah hay, chunanchay

Hazrat Sayyiduna Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُما farmatay hayn: jab Sarkar صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم Madinah Munawwarah tashreef laye to daykha kay Ghayr muslim A’ashura kay din rozah rakhtay hayn. Irshad farmaya: ye kiya hay? Unho nay ‘arz ki: yeh achha din hay. Us roz Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ nay Bani Israeel ko un kay dushman say najaat di thi to Hazrat Musa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم nay us ka rozah rakha. Aap صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم nay Irshad farmaya: tumhari nisbat mayra Hazrat Musa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم say Ziyada ta’alluq hay chunanchay Aap صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم nay Aashura ka rozah rakha aur is din rozah rakhnay ka hokum farmaya. (Bukhari, jild. 1, pp. 656, Hadees 2004)

Tirmizi Shareef may hay kay Hazrat Sayyiduna Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُما nay ye Aayt-e-Mubarakah tilawat farmaye:

اَلْیَوْمَ اَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِیْنَكُمْ وَ اَتْمَمْتُ عَلَیْكُمْ نِعْمَتِیْ وَ رَضِیْتُ لَكُمُ الْاِسْلَامَ دِیْنًاؕ-

Tarjama Kanz ul Iman: Aaj Mayn nay tumharay liye tumhara Deen kaamil kar diya aur tum par apni na’mat pori ker di aur tumharay liye islam ko Deen pasand kiya (Maaidah, Aayat. 3)

Ye aayat sun ker Aap رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ kay paas maujood aik ghayr muslim kahnay laga: agar ye aayat hum par naazil hoti to hum us kay naazil hony kay din eid ko manatay. Aap رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ nay Irshad farmaya: jis roz yeh aayat Mubarakah naazil huyi us din 2 eidayn thi: (1) Jumu’ah (2) Arafah. (Tirmizi, Hadees 3055)

Is say ma’loom huwa kay kisi Deeni kamyabi kay din ko khushi ka din manana jayz aur Sahabah kiram عَـلَيْهِمُ الرِّضْوَانْ say saabit hay warnah Aap رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ saaf farma daytay kay jis din koi khush ka waaqi’ah ho us ki yaad gar qaim kerna aur us roz ko eid manana hum bid’at jantay hayn, is say saabit huwa kay Eid Meelad manana jayz hgay kyun kay woh Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki sab say Azeem na’mat ki yaad gaar o shukr guzari hay. (Sirat ul Jinan, jild. 2, pp. 382)

Majlis Madrasa Tul Madnah online

Meethi meethi Islami behno! اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰہ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ Aashiqan-e-Rasool ki Madani tahreek Dawat-e-Islami ka Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki sab say bari na’mat ya’ni wiladat-e-mustafa ki shukar guzari kernay ka apna aik munfarid andaz hay, dunya kay bay shumar mumalik may Dawat-e-Islami kay zayr-e-ihtimam Eid Melaad-un-Nabi ki shab ko ‘Azeem-us-shan ijtima’-e- Meelad mun’aqid hota hay. Ijtima-e-meelad ki barakaton kay kiya kahnay! Is may shirkat kernay walay na janay kitnay hi khush naseebon ki zindagiyon may Madani inqilab barpa ho jata hay lihaza Jashn-e-Wiladat kay is fayzan say fayzyab honay kay liye aap bhi Dawat-e-Islami kay Madani mahool say wabastah rahiye aur khoob khoob Meelad-e-Mustafa ki dhoomayn machaye. اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰہ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ Dawat-e-Islami kam o baysh 104 shu’bah jaat may nayki ki da’wat aam kernay may masroof hay, jin may say aik shu’bah Madrasah tul Madinah online bhi hay. Shawwal ul Mukarram 1432 hijri September 2017 may Majlis Madrasa tul Madina Online ka Qiyam amal may laya gaya. Is shu’bay kay tahat internet kay zaree’ay mukhtalif mumalik kay Musalmanon ko na sirf durust makharij kay sath Quran pak ki muft ta’leem di jati hay balky bunyadi Islami ta’leemat wuzoo, ghusul Tayammum, azaan, namaaz rozah zakat aur hajj waghayrah kay masaail bhi sikhaye jatay hayn. Dawat-e-Islami ki website (www.dawateislami.net) par is shu’bay ki tafseeli ma’loomaat aur dakhilah forum bhi maujood hay, lihaza Madrasa tul Madinah online lil Banaat may dakhilay ki khuwahish mand Islami bahnay Dawat-e-Islami ki website ka zaroor visit karay.
