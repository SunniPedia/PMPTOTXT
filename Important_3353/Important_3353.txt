The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said: (Sayyiduna) Jibra`eel (عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام) came to him stating humbly that Allah (عَزَّوَجَلَّ) said: ‘O Muhammad (صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم)! Are you not pleased that when anyone from your Ummah sends Salat upon you once then I shower mercy ten times upon him, and when anyone from your Ummah sends one Salam, then I send Salams ten times upon him . ’ (Mishkat-ul-Masabih, vol. 1, pp. 189, Hadees 928)

Du’a of Rajab

Reciting the following Du’a in the blessed month of Rajab is an act of Sunnah; the Noblest Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم used to recite the following Du’a when the blessed month of Rajab would commence:

اَللّٰهُمَّ بَارِكۡ لَنَا فِيۡ رَجَبٍ وَّ شَعۡبَانَ وَ بَلِّغۡنَا رَمَضَان

Translation: O Allah (عَزَّوَجَلَّ)! Descend Your blessing upon us in the [blessed] months of Rajab and Sha’ban, and make us reach Ramadan.

(Al-Mu’jam-ul-Awsat, vol. 3, pp. 85, Hadees 3939)

Dear Islamic brothers! Before listening to the Bayan, let’s make good intentions for attaining rewards. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said, ‘ نِيَّةُ الۡمُؤۡمِنِ خَيۡرٌ مِّنۡ عَمَلِهٖ ’ The intention of a believer is better than his action .

(Al-Mu’jam-ul-Kabeer, vol. 6, pp. 185, Hadees 5942)

Two Madani pearls

Without a good intention, no reward is granted for a good deed.

The more righteous intentions one makes the greater reward he will attain.

Intentions of listening to the Bayan

Lowering my eyes, I will listen to the Bayan attentively.

Instead of resting against a wall etc., I will sit as I sit in Tashahhud as long as possible with the intention of showing respect for religious knowledge.

I will make room for others by folding my hands and limbs and by moving slightly.

If someone pushes me, I will remain patient and calm and avoid staring, snapping, and arguing with them.

When I hear صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب, اُذۡكُرُوا اللّٰه, تُوۡبُوۡا اِلَي اللّٰه, etc., I will reply loudly with the intention of gaining reward and encouraging others to also recite.

After the Bayan, I will approach other people by making Salam, shaking hands, and for making individual efforts upon them.

Great and Blessed Night:

Dear Islamic brothers! Tonight is the 27 th night of Rajab-ul-Murajjab 1438 AH. Millions of thanks to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ that He عَزَّوَجَلَّ has, once again, blessed us with the great and sacred night that is full of Divine blessings and virtues.

Dear Islamic brothers! Tonight is the great and glorious night in which a great miracle was bestowed upon our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . Tonight the chief of all the angels Sayyiduna Jibra`eel-e-Ameen عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام came to the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم with an extremely fastest ride ‘Buraaq’ [a white animal from Paradise]. The angels made the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم a bridegroom, and then he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was mounted onto the Buraaq with extreme respect and honour. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم instantly reached Bayt-ul-Muqaddas from Makkah where all the Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام welcomed him. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم led the Salah of all the Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام . Tonight he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم toured all the skies and saw their wonders. Tonight he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم blessed the Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام with his sacred sight in every sky. The Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام congratulated the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم upon his arrival. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم reached Sidra-tul-Muntaha. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم reached the place from where no one else can dare to move further, even Sayyiduna Jibra`eel-e-Ameen عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام also mentioned his inability to move further. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم reached the place beyond the boundaries of intellect and wisdom. Tonight the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was blessed with Divine gifts; special gifts were bestowed upon the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ

وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . Tonight, initially the gift of 50 Salahs was first bestowed upon our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم from the court of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, which were reduced to 5 Salahs and these 5 Salahs were made Fard (compulsory) for us. Tonight the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم toured the Paradise and Hell. Tonight he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم attained the special closeness to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and was privileged to see the Divine vision of the Creator عَزَّوَجَلَّ and the Owner of the whole universe with his own blessed eyes.

Who can fulfil the right of mentioning the greatness of Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم ? Anyway, listen to it with all ears and ease of mind. اِنْ شَــآءَال ـ لّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ the love of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم will grow further in the heart.

Parable of a disciple of Ibn-e-Sakinah:

Narrating a parable Shaykh Shahabuddin Suharwardi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه stated that a goldsmith was a disciple of Shaykh Ibn-e-Sakinah رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه . His task was to take the prayer mats of pious saints رَحِمَهُمُ الـلّٰـهُ تَـعَالٰی to Jami’ Masjid and to spread them. After the Friday Salah had been performed he would bring the prayer mats back to the monastery. One Friday, he tied the prayer mats together for taking them to the Jami’ Masjid and went to the river Tigris [in Baghdad] to perform the Ghusl for Friday Salah. He took off his clothes and kept them along the side of the river and dived into the water. When he took his head out of the water, he did not find himself in the river Tigris, but somewhere else. He asked people about the place and was told that it was Egypt. He was very surprised at this [incident]; he came out of the water and went to the city; over there he stood before the shop of a goldsmith. He was wearing only one piece of cloth that was sufficient to cover his Satr [parts of body which are compulsory to be concealed]. The shopkeeper felt that this person was a goldsmith. During the conversation the shopkeeper realized that he was very expert in this art. The shopkeeper treated him with great respect and took him to his home and performed his Nikah (marriage) with his daughter. Then three children were born in seven years. One day, the disciple passed by the

same water, he dived into the water for taking bath. When he took his head out of the water he found himself at the same place of the river Tigris where he dived seven years ago. He saw his clothes lying along the side of the river in the same manner as he placed them. He wore his clothes and went to the monastery. When he reached the monastery, he found the prayer mats in the same condition. One of his fellow disciples told him: Hurry up, some people have gone to Jami’ Masjid early in the morning. He then took the prayer mats to Jami’ Masjid, offered Salah and returned to the monastery. Surprised and worried, he went to his home from the monastery. His wife asked: Where are the people for whom you asked me to fry the fish; the fish is ready. He brought the guests and all ate the fish.

He then went to his Shaykh, Ibn-e-Sakinah رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه and related the whole story. He also talked about his offspring in Egypt. Shaykh ordered him to go to Egypt to bring his wife and children back. That person brought his wife and children back. After this the Shaykh asked: Did you have any evil-whisper in your heart? He gave a positive reply by saying that he had an illusion about Mi’raaj as to how the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم physically travelled so long distance in such a short period of time. The Shaykh replied: It is the blessing of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ that He has removed your doubts.

(Tuhfa-e-Mi’raaj-un-Nabi, pp. 82)

Dear Islamic brothers! Have you noticed! The slave of the slaves of the slaves of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم dives into the river Tigris of Baghdad at noon in the state of wakefulness, and by the Divine bestowment of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, finds himself in Egypt when takes his head out of the water, so then what will be the height of greatness and glory of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! The caliph of A’la Hazrat ‘Allamah Maulana Zafaruddin Bihari رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said that the event of the minister of Sayyiduna Sulayman عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم Sayyiduna Asif bin Barkhiya رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه (who was a saint) is stated in the Holy Quran that he humbly said to Sayyiduna Sulayman عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم: I will bring the

throne of queen Bilqees to you before the blink of your eye and then he did the same. The throne of Bilqees was 80 yards long and 80 yards high and was made of gold and silver studded with extremely heavy and precious diamonds and jewels. When a pious saint by his saintly miracle brings such a large and heavy throne from Yemen to Syria before the blink of an eye, so it is certainly possible for the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to visit with his body [from Masjid-e-Haram to Masjid-e-Aqsa and from there to the skies in a short part of night]. Dear Islamic brothers! Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has bestowed many miracles upon His Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم which cannot be counted. All the miracles which were bestowed upon other Ambiya عَـلَيْـهِمُ الـسَّـلاَم separately were also bestowed upon him besides many other miracles.

Out of these thousands of miracles of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم the most famous miracle and the most unique event of the universe is the blessed Mi’raaj. In this event, our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم went to Masjid-e-Aqsa from Masjid-e-Haram and then from Masjid-e-Aqsa he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم went on the tour of the skies. In fact, all this journey of Mi’raaj is revealing the greatness of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . On the occasion of setting off on the journey of Mi’raaj, the blessed chest of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was cut open. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم led the Salah of all the Ambiya and Rusul عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام on this blessed journey. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ

وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was blessed with the Divine vision of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ . In short, journey of Mi’raaj is such a great miracle of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم that its example is not found in the history. It makes the glory and greatness of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and his status and rank in the Divine court of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ more crystal clear than the sun. Let’s have the privilege of listening to the different aspects of the event of Mi’raaj and the glory and greatness of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم on the journey of Mi’raaj. How elevated Allah عَزَّوَجَلّ َ made the glory of His Habeeb:

An excellent aspect of the greatness of Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم on the journey of Mi’raaj is that Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has mentioned this journey of His

Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم in the Holy Quran with such a glory that attributing the act of making him travel so long in a short part of night to Himself He عَزَّوَجَلَّ has stated:

سُبْحٰنَ الَّذِیْۤ اَسْرٰى بِعَبْدِهٖ لَیْلًا مِّنَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ اِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ الْاَقْصَا Holy is He Who carried His bondman by night from the sacred Masjid to the Aqsa Masjid.

[Kanz-ul-Iman (Translation of Quran)](Part 15, Surah Bani Israel, Ayah 1)

Regarding this blessed Ayah, Maulana Mufti Muhammad Na’eemuddin Muradabadi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has stated: The blessed Mi’raaj is a great miracle of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and a great blessing of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ . It reveals the miraculous closeness of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم [to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ] which no other being of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ except the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has ever attained. (He رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه further said) It is the belief of the glorious blessed companions of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم that blessed Mi’raaj occurred in the state of wakefulness [instead of dream] with body and soul both. (Khazain-ul-Irfan, pp. 525)

Dear Islamic brothers! Remember, though the journey from Masjid-e-Haram to Masjid-e-Aqsa has been mentioned in this blessed Ayah, the journey of blessed Mi’raaj of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم did not end in Masjid-e-Aqsa [started from Masjid-e-Haram], but rather not only did our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم toured all the seven skies on the night of Mi’raaj, but he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم also went farther ahead to the place where Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ wanted him to.

In Fatawa Razawiyyah, A’la Hazrat, Imam-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Maulana Shah Imam Ahmad Raza Khan عَـلَيْهِ رَحْمَةُ الـرَّحْمٰن has narrated: The privilege of conversation was bestowed upon Sayyiduna Musa عَـلٰى نَبِـيِّنَا وَعَـلَيْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام, our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was blessed with the same in the Isra Night [the night of Mi’raaj]. Besides having conversation with Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, our

Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was also blessed with extreme closeness to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and His Divine vision with his own blessed eyes. Of course, how could one compare Koh-e-Toor, on which Sayyiduna Musa عَـلٰى نَبِـيِّنَا وَعَـلَيْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام implored to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, with the place that is even above the Arsh where a Divine conversation was held with the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم!

He رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه further said: On the night of Mi’raaj, our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم in the state of wakefulness physically visited the skies, then Sidra-tul-Muntaha, then Maqaam-e-Mustawa, then ‘Arsh and Rafraf and then had the privilege to behold the Divine vision of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ .

(Fatawa Razawiyyah, vol. 30, pp. 646)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Event of cutting the blessed chest open:

Dear Islamic brothers! Another aspect of the greatness and glory of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is that prior to setting off for Bayt-ul-Muqaddas during the Journey of Mi’raaj, his blessed chest was cut open in Hateem-e-Ka’bah. As a perfect pious saint, ‘Allamah Maulana Makhdoom Muhammad Hashim Thathavi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: It has been narrated that on the blessed night of Mi’raaj, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام cut open the blessed chest of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . Then his blessed heart was taken out and given Ghusl with Zam Zam water by putting it in a gold salver that was full of faith and Divine knowledge. Then [as per the glory and status of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم ] further Divine knowledge, faith and the luminosity of Nubuwwah were filled into it and then after putting them into the blessed chest it was sewn with a needle.

Islamic scholars رَحِمَهُمُ الـلّٰـهُ تَـعَالٰی have said that the blessed chest of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was cut open four times in his sacred life. The event that occurred on the night of Mi’raaj was the fourth one.

(Seerat Sayyid-ul-Ambiya, pp. 127)

Wisdom behind cutting chest open:

Explaining the reasons for cutting the blessed chest open of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم Shaykh-ul-Hadees ‘Allamah Maulana Abdul Mustafa A’zami رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: The blessed chest of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was cut open four times. First time, it occurred when he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was in the house of Haleemah Sadiyyah رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا . It was aimed at protecting him from the evil thoughts that attract children to games and mischievous activities. Second time, when the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was 10 years old his blessed chest was cut open so that he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم would remain unaffected and unharmed by the risks of the desires of youth. Third time, his blessed chest was cut open in the cave Hira where his blessed heart was filled with Noor and peace so that he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم could bear the great burden of the revelation from the Almighty. Fourth time, on the night of Mi’raaj, his blessed chest was cut open and filled with the treasure of Noor and Divine wisdom so that his blessed heart may have the magnitude and capability to bear the Tajalliyaat of Divine sight and the greatness of Divine conversation. (Seerat-e- Mustafa, pp. 79-80)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

The blessed conveyance of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم:

Dear Islamic brothers! Another aspect of the greatness of Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم in the event of Mi’raaj is that for this great and glorious journey, extremely great Heavenly conveyance was selected for the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . Sayyiduna Anas bin Malik رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ has narrated that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said: Buraaq [whose name was Jarood.] was brought to me which was extremely white and tall quadruped. It was bigger than a donkey and smaller than a mule. It could place its hooves at the farthest boundary of its gaze. (Muslim, pp. 87, Hadees, 411)

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: Here [in Hateem-e-Ka’bah] blessed chest was cut open; Ghusl was given with Kausar and Zam Zam; the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was made a bridegroom after being adorned

with a Heavenly dress and then the angels accompanied the spiritual procession with the Beloved Dignitary of Mi’raaj صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . Sayyiduna Jibra’eel-e-Ameen عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم helped him mount Buraaq; Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم held the stirrup and Sayyiduna Miqa’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم held the reins and the blessed conveyance of the bridegroom started moving with great glory. Remember, the act of mounting Buraaq by the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was to show the glory as a bridegroom is on the horse and Baarati [people attending marriage procession] are on foot. This Buraaq was especially for the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was also riding it on the journey of Mi’raaj and will also be riding it on the Judgement Day. It grazes in Paradise. Every Nabi عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم will have a separate Buraaq for conveyance in Paradise, but the Buraaq of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم will be the best one and it is the same Buraaq.

(Mirat-ul-Manajeeh, pp. 136-137)

The grand blessed conveyance of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was so beautiful and its speed was so fast that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم himself stated about its amazing speed: Its step (was so wide that it) reached the farthest point within the reach of its sight. This means, covering the distance of days in moments our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم instantly reached Masjid-e-Aqsa from Masjid-e-Haram, the distance of which was more than a month’s time.

Remember! only the part of the first and famous blessed verse of Surah Bani Isra’eel in which the event of Mi’raaj has been mentioned instead of Heavenly journey is itself an extremely wonderful Prophetic miracle as there was a considerable distance between Masjid-ul-Haraam and Masjid-ul-Aqsa,

therefore, for a commoner to go to Masjid-ul-Aqsa overnight and then return during the same night is something totally impossible, even it was not possible to cover the distance of one side in one night. Allamah Isma’eel Haqqi عَـلَيْهِ رَحْـمَةُ الـلّٰـهِ الۡـقَـوِی has said: [Journey] To Aqsa has been mentioned because, in those days, there was no other Masjid so far away as Masjid-ul-Aqsa; it was Masjid-ul-Aqsa that was the farthest Masjid from Makkah; there was a distance of more than a month between Masjid-ul-Haraam and Masjid-ul-Aqsa. (Ruh-ul-Bayan, part 15 Surah Bani Israel, Taht-al-Ayah 1, vol. 5, pp. 114)

You are the Imam (Leader) of Ambiya:

Another aspect of the greatness of Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم on the journey of Mi’raaj is that our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم on that night, led the Salah of all the blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام and blessed them to become his Muqtadi. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said: Riding on it [Buraaq] I reached Bayt-ul-Muqaddas and the place where the blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام would tether their riding-animals. I tethered it there. Then I entered the Masjid [Aqsa] and offered two Rak’aat Salah. (Sahih Muslim, Kitab-ul-Imaan, pp. 97, Hadees 259)

Wali-e-Kaamil, ‘Allamah Maulana Makhdoom Muhammad Hashim Thathavi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was the Imam of all the Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام in this Salah [1] [as] all the Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام were gathered in Bait-ul-Muqaddas so that they could be shown the great status and glory of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام then held the blessed hand of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and requested to move forward and then the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم led the Salah of all the Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام . (Al-Mu’jam-ul-Awsat lit-Tabarani, vol. 3, pp. 65, Hadees 3879)

On Mi’raaj night, the Salah, in which the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم led all the Ambiya and great Rusul عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام in Masjid Aqsa, it contained a secret and the secret was to differentiate between the first and the last (so that it can become obvious that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم who came into the world as the last Nabi is not less than any other Nabi in glory

and greatness). Sermons delivered by Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام:

Dear Islamic brothers! Another aspect of the greatness of Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم during the journey of Mi’raaj is that not only did our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم state the excellent glory and marvels of himself and his Ummah before the Ambiya in Bait-ul-Muqaddas, but Sayyiduna Ibrahim Khaleelullah عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم also mentioned the highly elevated rank of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . After the Salah that was offered in Bait-ul-Muqaddas some glorious Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام delivered sermons in which they praised Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and mentioned the blessings and mercy which Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ bestowed upon them.

First of all, Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم delivered the sermon, then Sayyiduna Musa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم . After him Sayyiduna Dawood عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم delivered the sermon, then Sayyiduna Sulayman عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم . After Sayyiduna Sulayman عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم Sayyiduna Isa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم delivered the sermon.

After the blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام had praised Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and mentioned the Divine mercy and blessings which Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ had bestowed upon them, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم delivered the sermon: Before delivering the sermon, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said to Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام: You all have mentioned the praise of your Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ and now I will mention the praise of my Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ . Then praising Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and mentioning his own glory the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said:

All the praise are due to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, Who made me merciful for the whole worlds and sent me after making me the one who tells good news and fear for all the human beings and revealed the Book [Quran] to me which differentiates the right with the wrong, in which everything has been clearly

mentioned and made my Ummah the best and superior to all other Ummahs. My Ummah has been made the first and the last (there will be no other Ummah after my Ummah, it is the last Ummah and from the perspective of entering Paradise, my Ummah will be the first amongst all other Ummahs) and Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ expanded my chest for me, removed burden from me, dignified my remembrance and made me victorious and Khatim (the last Nabi).

Having heard the great virtues of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلٰى نَبِـيِّنَا وَعَـلَيْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام said to Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام: بِہٰذَا فَضَلَکُمْ مُحَمَّدٌ By virtue of these great attributes Muhammad صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has excellence and superiority over all of you. (Dalail-ul-Nubuwwah lil-Bayhaqi, vol. 2, pp. 400)

Special door of Sky was opened for Mi’raaj:

Dear Islamic brothers! The glory and greatness of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم during the journey of Mi’raaj can also be judged by the fact that there are many doors in the sky which are opened at different times for different types of work. One of them was opened only on Mi’raaj night especially for the honour and respect of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . That door had neither been opened for anyone else before, nor will it be opened for anyone else after the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم .

Sayyiduna Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ has narrated that Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم was sitting near the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم (on Mi’raaj night). The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم, meanwhile, heard a voice from the sky. As he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم heard the voice he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم raised his blessed head. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم humbly said: O the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم, the door that has been opened is the door of the sky which had never been opened before. Then an angel came down through the door. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم humbly said: It is the angel that had never come down to the earth before. Having said Salam in the court of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم the angel humbly said: I give you good news of two such Noors which had never been bestowed upon anyone except you before (1) Surah Fatihah (2) Last Ayahs of Surah Baqarah. You will be rewarded for every letter of these both. (Muslim, pp. 314, Hadees, 1877)

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: The sky has millions of doors through which different things come and go. Sustenance comes through some doors, torment descends through some doors, Du’as and repentance ascend through some doors, special angels come down through some doors; there is a door which was only opened for the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم on the night of Mi’raaj. (Mirat-ul-Manajeeh, vol. 8, pp. 138)

Whatever Du’a is made after reciting Surah Fatihah 100 times Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ accepts it. (Jannati Zaywar, pp. 58)

Satan will not come near the house for three days in which the two Ayahs [last two Ayahs of Surah Al-Baqarah] will be recited. (Al-Mustadrak, pp. 268, vol. 2, Hadees / Raqm 2109)

Whoever recites the last two Ayahs of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, these will be suffice him.’ (Sahih Bukhari, pp. 405, vol. 3, Hadees 5009)

Ascending towards the Sky:

Another aspect of the glory and greatness of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم during the journey of Mi’raaj is that he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم also toured the skies and over there angels and different blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام warmly welcomed him. After the activities in Bayt-tul-Muqaddas, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم started the journey towards the sky and continued to move swiftly towards the sky and arrived at the first sky within no time.

It is stated in a blessed Hadees that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم arrived at one of its doors called Bab-ul-Hafazah. An angel named Ismail is appointed there. (‘Umdah-tul-Qaari. Vol. 11, pp. 603, That-al-Hadees, 3887) Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم wanted the door to be opened. It was asked: Who is this? Sayyiduna Jibra’eel-e-Ameen عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم replied: I am Jibra’eel. He was asked:

Who is with you? He replied: He is Muhammad-e-Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . It was then asked: Has he (صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم) been called? He replied: Yes. The door was opened saying this: مَرْحَبًا م بِہٖ فَنِعْمَ الْمَجِیْءُ جَاءَ i.e. Welcome! How good is the one who has arrived! (Bukhari, vol. 2, pp. 584, Hadees, 3887)

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: The announcement of the arrival of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم had already been made to the angels and the skies had been decorated in every way, the news of the arrival had been spread everywhere. That night millions of angels came to take the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم in Makkah Mu’azzamah and many angels were carrying out their duties to welcome. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel-e-Ameen صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم took the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم through the door which had not been opened for anyone before [today]. It was only opened for the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم (despite knowing all these things and despite the angles being aware of the blessed arrival of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم) the three questions of the sky-doorkeeper angel (Who are you? Who is with you? And has he been called?) are only for the purpose that this door is not for you, Jibra’eel. Your door is other than this. Why do you want to enter here today? He replied that today I am with the one for whom this door has been made and kept closed. Today is the day to open this door, therefore these questions were only asked to fulfil the legal and procedural measures. (Mirat-ul-Manajeeh, vol. 8, pp. 138)

When the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم went above the sky after passing through the door, he saw Sayyiduna Adam عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام sitting. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام humbly said: He is your father Sayyiduna Adam عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام . Please say Salaam to him. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said Salaam and he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام replied to his Salaam and then expressed his joy upon his arrival in these words. مَرْحَبًا م بِالْاِبْنِ الصَّالِحِ وَالنَّبِیِّ الصَّالِحِ i.e. Welcome to the righteous son and righteous Nabi. (Sahih Bukhari, vol. 2, pp. 584, Hadees 3887)

Similarly, questions and answers were carried out on arrival at every sky and the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم met blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام . For instance, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw Sayyiduna Yahya and Sayyiduna Isa عَـلَـيْهِمَا الـسَّـلَام in the second sky; they both are maternal cousins. In the third sky, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw Sayyiduna Yusuf عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام ; Sayyiduna Idrees عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام in the fourth sky. Sayyiduna Haroon عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام in the fifth sky and Sayyiduna Musa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام in the sixth sky and then in the seventh sky he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام who was sitting leaning against Bait-ul-Mamoor. (All these Ambiya welcomed the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم .)

(Bukhari, vol. 2, pp. 584, Hadees, 3887)

Sidra-tul-Muntaha

On page 34 of ‘Faizan-e-Mi’raaj’ a 134-page book published by Maktaba tul Madinah it is stated: Having met Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلٰى نَبِـيِّنَا وَعَـلَيْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام in the seventh sky, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم reached near Sidra-tul-Muntaha. (Sahih Bukhari, vol.2, pp.584, Hadees 3887)

It is a luminous jujube tree whose root is in the sixth sky and branches on the seventh sky. (Bukhari, vol. 2, pp. 584, Hadees, 3887) ; its fruits are as large as the large earthen pots of Hajr [city of Yemen] and leaves are like the ears of the elephant. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام humbly said: It is Sidra-tul-Muntaha. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw four streams here which used to come out of the root of Sidra-tul-Muntaha; two of them were visible and two were hidden. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم asked Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام: O Jibra’eel! What type of streams are these? Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام humbly said: Hidden streams are from Paradise (these are Heavenly streams—Kausar and Sal-Sabeel or Kausar and stream of Mercy Ref) and the visible streams are the Nile and Furaat.

(Sahih Bukhari, vol. 2, pp. 584, Hadis 3887)

Special attachment to Sha’ban-ul-Mu’azzam

Excellence of Siyam in Sha’ban

After the Fard Siyam of Ramadan, the next most preferable Siyam declared by our Beloved and Blessed Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم are those of Sha’ban. As the books of deeds of all people are conveyed to the Divine court in this month, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم himself observed Siyam in abundance in this month, motivating also his Ummah to observe Siyam in it. Let’s now have the privilege of listening to three sayings of Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم about the excellence of Siyam in Sha’ban-ul-Mu’azzam.

1. The Nabi of mankind, the Peace of our heart and mind, the most Generous and Kind صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was humbly asked: ‘Which Sawm is preferable after [i.e. excluding] Ramadan?’ He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied,

‘Observing Sawm in Shaban for honouring Ramadan.’ (Shu’ab-ul-Iman, vol. 3, pp. 377, Hadees 3819)

1. Sayyidatuna ‘Ayesha Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا has narrated, ‘The Beloved and Blessed Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم would keep Siyam throughout Sha’ban.’ She رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا once asked, ‘Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! Do you like it the most to observe Siyam in Sha’ban out of all [other] months?’ He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied, ‘Yes, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ records [the name of] every living being who will die this year and I like to be in the state of Sawm at the time of my demise.’ (Musnad Abi Ya’la, vol. 4, pp. 277, Hadees 4890)

2. The Beloved and Blessed Prophet صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, ‘This month is between Rajab and Ramadan; people are heedless of it. It is the month in which people’s deeds are lifted towards Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ . I wish that my deed be lifted when I am in the state of Sawm.’ (Shu’ab-ul-Iman, vol. 3, pp. 377, Hadees 3820)

Dear Islamic brothers! Just ponder! How great acts of worship were performed by our Beloved Rasul صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم who is already forgiven and will get us – the sinners – forgiven. 15 th Shaban. We should also pray to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ to be blessed with goodness in our worldly life as well as in our afterlife.

Mi’raaj of Mu’min

Dear Islamic brothers! On this night, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ blessed His Beloved Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم with Mi’raaj [i.e. Ascension] and called him to Laa-Makaan where a great gift of a great act of worship, i.e. Salah, was bestowed upon him. This gift of Salah is the Mi’raaj of the Ummah for the sake of the Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . A commentator of the Quran, Allamah Isma’eel Haqqi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has stated, ‘Without doubt, Salah is the Mi’raaj of Mu’min.’ (Ruh-ul-Bayan, Part.

13, pp. 429) Therefore, we should value this Divinely-bestowed great gift and should always offer Salah regularly. This will bring countless blessings to our worldly life and afterlife. Moreover, there is also a tremendous benefit of it. By the grace and benevolence of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, we will be granted reward tenfold for each of the five Salahs. Therefore, make a firm intention of offering Salah regularly from today. If a person only makes a true intention of a good deed, a virtue is recorded for him. And if he performs the good deed later on acting upon his good intention, so ten virtues are recorded for him. On this sacred night, let’s now hear about Salah being declared Fard as well as about its excellence and about the blessing of good intention.

Obligation of Salah and importance of intention

The Greatest and Noblest Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said: On the night of Mi’raaj, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ declared 50 Salahs Fard (obligatory) for me during a day and night. When I reached Sayyiduna Musa عَـلٰى نَبِـيِّنَا وَعَـلَيْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام, he said: What has your Lord عَزَّوَجَلَّ declared Fard for your Ummah? I said: 50 Salahs were declared Fard during every day and night. Sayyiduna Musa عَـلٰى نَبِـيِّنَا وَعَـلَيْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام said: Go to your Lord عَزَّوَجَلَّ and get the number of Salahs decreased, because your Ummah will not be able to offer 50 Salahs. I have already tested Bani Isra’eel. (Only two Salahs were declared Fard for the Bani Isra’eel during the day and night but they could not offer them. So how will your Ummah be able to offer fifty Salahs? The Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم stated, I returned to my Lord عَزَّوَجَلَّ and humbly said: O my Lord (عَزَّوَجَلَّ)! Grant some easiness to my Ummah. Dear Islamic brothers! Let’s hear more about greatness of the Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم during the journey of Mi’raaj. Another fact that shows reverence for the Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم during the journey of Mi’raaj is that by the sake of His beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ resolved the difficulties and issues being faced by His high-ranking angels for a long time.

It is narrated that high-ranking angels had been pondering over four issues for four thousand years but they could not find any solution to them. When the Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم came in the world, those angels were sure that the difficult issues could only be resolved by him. Therefore, all of those angels requested Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ with extreme humility and humbleness to get their issues resolved. So Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ called His Beloved صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم towards Him, blessing him with the privilege of His closeness and of having Kalam with Him. The Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has stated, ‘I saw my Lord when I was in an extremely beautiful form. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ said, ‘O Beloved [Nabi] صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! Which are the issues the highly-ranking angels are stuck on?’ I humbly said, ‘O my Lord! You know those issues the best.’ Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ placed His matchless Dast-e-Qudrat between both of my shoulders, so I felt the effect of its coolness in my chest. Then (Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ said again), ‘O Beloved [Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم ]! Which are the issues the high-ranking angels have been pondering about (and have not resolved them)?’ I humbly said, ‘Angels have been pondering about four things: (1) Kaffaraat (2) Munjiyyaat (3) Darajaat (4) Muhlikaat.’ Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ said, ‘O My Beloved! You have told the truth.’ Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ then said to the angels, ‘O My angels! You have got the opportunity to get your difficulty resolved. Put your question.’ Therefore, Sayyiduna Izra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم humbly asked, ‘What are Kaffaraat? (i.e. the deeds due to which Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ removes the sins of bondmen.) The Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, ‘There are three deeds (that cause sins to be removed): (1) Making complete Wudu in extreme cold (i.e. completely washing the parts of body that are washed in Wudu so that Wudu is perfect) (2) Walking [to

Masjid] with the intention of offering Salah with congregation (3) Waiting for the other Salah after a person has offered one Salah (these 3 acts are the source of removing sins). Then, Sayyiduna Mika’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم humbly asked, ‘What are Darajaat? (i.e. the deeds that cause the ranks of a person to be raised). The Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, ‘Feeding food to the hungry for the pleasure of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ; promoting Salam among people and offering Nafl Salah at night when people are asleep. (These three deeds cause ranks to be raised in the Divine court.) Then, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم humbly said, ‘What are Munjiyyaat? (i.e. the deeds which, if performed, protect from torment.) The Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, ‘Fearing Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ apparently as well as innerly; practicing moderation in poverty as well as in prosperity; doing justice in anger as well as in calmness (these 3 deeds bring salvation). Then Sayyiduna Izra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم humbly asked, ‘What are Muhlikaat? (i.e. the deeds that cause a person to be doomed.) The Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, (1) ‘Following (i.e. showing) miserliness; (2) following desire of Nafs; (3) and considering oneself to be better than others (these 3 deeds cause a person to be doomed). Then Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ said, ‘My Beloved has told the truth’

Dear Islamic brothers! You have just heard how great respect and reverence was bestowed upon the Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم by Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ . We should always sing the praises of our Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and spend the rest of our life performing good deeds abundantly, refraining from sins and acting upon his Sunnah, showing our devotion to him. Bear in mind, on the night of Mi’raaj, our Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw the obedient people being blessed with Divine favours in Heaven but we should remember that he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم also saw the disobedient ones being afflicted with Divine wrath in Hell. The sinners were being punished very severely for their sins. Let’s hear about some of those punishments so that we learn some lesson.

People eating their own flesh

On the night of Mi’raaj, the Greatest and Noblest Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم passed by some people. There were also some other people [who were

punishing] them. Some of the [punishing] people opened the jaws of those [who were being punished] and some others cut their flesh and pushed it into their mouth with blood. The Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم asked, ‘O Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم! Who are these people?’ He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم humbly replied, ‘They indulged in backbiting of people and involved in finding their faults.’ (Musnad-ul-Haris, vol. 1, pp. 172, Hadees 27)

Snakes in stomach

The Beloved and Blessed Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has stated, ‘On the night of Mi’raaj, I came to some people whose stomachs were (big) like houses with snakes in them which were also visible from the outside of their stomachs. I asked Jibra’eel (عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام), ‘Who are these people?’ He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم replied, ‘They used to take usury [i.e. interest on money, etc.].’ (Sunan Ibn Majah, pp. 363, Hadees 2273)

Head-crushing punishment

On the night of Mi’raaj, the Beloved and Blessed Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم came to some people whose heads were being crushed with stones. (After being crushed) their heads healed every time (and were crushed once again). No leniency was shown to them in this matter. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم asked Jibra’eel (عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام), ‘Who are these people?’ He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم replied, ‘These are the people who would offer Salah unwillingly. (Majma-us-Zawaid, vol. 1, pp. 92, Hadees, 235)

People hanging on the branches of fire

The Holy Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, ‘On the night of Mi’raaj, I saw some people who were hanging on the branches of fire. So I asked, ‘O Jibra`eel, who are these people?’ He replied: These are the people who would swear at their parents in the world.’ (Az-Zawajir, vol. 2, pp. 125)

Dear Islamic brothers! You listened. On the night of Mi’raaj, the Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw that those who would indulge in backbiting, take usury, miss Salah and swear at their parents were also being punished in Hell. So we

should repent of sins today from the bottom of our heart and make a firm intention of spending the rest of our life performing good deeds.

A Mu’takif resembles those (i.e. the angels) who do not disobey the command of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and carry out His every command; he resembles those who glorify Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ day and night and never get tired of doing so.’ (Fatawa ‘Alamgiri, vol. 1, pp. 212)

During I’tikaf, in addition, Wudu, Ghusl, Salah, Sawm and other Shari rulings are taught. One also gains a wealth of knowledge by attending two Madani Muzakarahs daily in which Shaykh-e-Tareeqat Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat دَامَـتْ بَـرَكَـاتُـهُـمُ الْـعَـالِـيَـهْ answers different types of questions in an interesting way, providing the attendees with knowledge and wisdom. Many fortunate lovers of Ramadan also get the privilege of attending various Madani courses such as Islah-e-Amaal course, 12-day Madani work course, Faizan-e-Namaz course, Imamat course, Mudarris course, etc.

Reward of Siyam of 100 years

Great excellence of the Sawm of the 27 th of Rajab has been narrated. The Greatest and Noblest Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, ‘There is a day and a night in Rajab. If a person observes Sawm during that day and stands (for worship) during the night, it is as if he has observed Siyam for one hundred years and worshipped at night for one hundred years; and that is the 27 th of Rajab.’ (Shu’ab-ul-Iman, vol. 3, pp. 374, Hadees 3811)

The month of Shaban is also approaching. There is also great excellence of Siyam in this month.

The Nabi of mankind, the Peace of our heart and mind, the most Generous and Kind صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said, ‘(After Ramadan), the most preferable Siyam are those of Sha’ban; for honouring Ramadan.’ (Shu’ab-ul-Iman, vol. 3, pp. 377, Hadees 3819)

Sayyidatuna ‘Ayesha Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا has narrated, ‘The Beloved and Blessed Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم would keep Siyam throughout Sha’ban.’ She رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا once asked, ‘Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! Do you like it to observe

Siyam in Sha’ban the most, out of all [other] months?’ He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied, ‘Yes, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ records [the name of] every living being who will die this year and I like to be in the state of Sawm at the time of my demise.’ (Musnad Abi Ya’la, vol. 4, pp. 277, Hadees 4890)

May Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ enable us to observe tomorrow’s Nafl Sawm as well as the Siyam of Shaban and then all the Siyam of Ramadan; for the sake of Makki Madani Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! If only we would succeed in attending I’tikaf for the entire month of Ramadan or for the last ten days! If only we also have the privilege of collecting Madani donations with good intentions.
