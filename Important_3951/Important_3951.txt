Excellence of Salat-‘Alan-Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم

The Rasool of Rahmah, the Intercessor of Ummah صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has stated: ‘ زَیِّنُوْا مَجَا لِسَکُمْ بِا لصَّلَا ۃِ عَلَیَّ فَاِنَّ صَلَاَ تَکُمْ عَلَیَّ نُوْ رٌ لَّکُمْ یَوْمَ ا لقِیَامَۃِ ’ Beautify your gatherings by reciting Salat upon me; because the Salat that you recite upon me, will be Noor for you on the Day of Judgement. (Al-Jami’-us-Sagheer, pp. 280, Hadees 4580)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Dear Islamic sisters! Before listening to the Bayan, let’s make good intentions for attaining rewards. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said, ‘ نِيَّةُ الۡمُؤۡمِنِ خَيۡرٌ مِّنۡ عَمَلِه ٖ ’ The intention of a believer is better than his action .

(Al-Mu’jam-ul-Kabeer, vol. 6, pp. 185, Hadees 5942)

Two Madani pearls

1 Without a good intention, no reward is granted for a good deed.

2 The more righteous intentions one makes the greater reward she will attain.

Note: ​ The intentions mentioned below can be modified as per situation.

1 Lowering my eyes, I will listen to the Bayan attentively.

2 Instead of resting against a wall etc., I will sit in reverential posture like that in Tashahhud as long as possible with the intention of paying respect to the religious discourse.

3 I will make room for other Islamic sisters by folding my hands and limbs and by moving slightly.

4 If someone pushes me, I will remain patient and calm and avoid staring, snapping, and arguing with them.

5 When I hear صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب, اُذۡكُرُوا اللّٰه, تُوۡبُوۡا اِلَي اللّٰه, etc., I will reply in low voice with the intention of gaining reward and encouraging others to also recite.

6 After Ijtima, I will take the lead to say Salam and shake hands and make individual effort.

7 During the speech, I will avoid the unnecessary use of mobile phone.

8 Neither will I record the speech, nor any kind of voice as it is not permitted.

9 Whatever I listen I will act upon it and later on convey it to others. In this way, I will be privileged to propagate the call towards righteousness.

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Dear Islamic sisters! Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has made Salah obligatory for us and promised us great blessings and gifts in the world and the Hereafter for offering Salah. When our pious predecessors would offer Salah, they would be present in the blessed court of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ with apparent and spiritual manners after being totally carefree of worldly thoughts and their surroundings. Let’s listen to the condition of these righteous people’s Salah.

Devotee of Salah and Holy Quran

Talking about the spiritual exercises and acts of worship of the blessed companions رَضِىَ الـلّٰـهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُم of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and relating the event of two blessed companions, Sayyiduna Jabir رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ has stated:

Once, we went to a Ghazwah (battle) along with the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . On the way back, we passed through a mountainous area where the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم ordered us to stay. All the blessed companions رَضِىَ الـلّٰـهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُم stayed there to rest. The Beloved and Blessed Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, ‘Who amongst you will keep a watch tonight?’ A Muhajir and an Ansari companion رَضِىَ الـلّٰـهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُمَا stood up and humbly said: Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! We would like to have this privilege; please accept us. They both got ready to keep watch after taking permission.

Both consulted with each other and then Ansari Sahabi رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ said, ‘Let’s do one thing-one of us will keep watch in the first half of the night while the other is asleep, then the other one will keep watch the remaining half of the night while the first one is asleep.’ Therefore, you rest while I am awake, then you keep watch.

Thus, the Muhajir companion رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ started having a rest and the Ansari companion رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ got ready to keep watch. After a while, he started offering Salah and reciting Surah Al-Kahf. An enemy came and climbed onto a hilltop and saw that a person was offering Salah; he drew his bow, and taking an aim fired an arrow to the blessed companion. The arrow pierced his body, but he رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ did not move and remained busy offering Salah. That cruel enemy shot another arrow that also entered his blessed body, but he رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ did not break his Salah. He then shot the third arrow which also came straight

and pierced his body after injuring him. He رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ performed Ruku’ and Sujood and completed his Salah. After completing Salah he woke his companion up. When the infidel saw that the blessed companion رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ was not alone, but his companions were also near him, he immediately ran away.

When the Muhajir companion رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ saw this condition of his companion, he took out the arrows quickly and asked, ‘Why didn’t you wake me when the enemy attacked you?’ The Ansari companion رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ (who was a devotee of Quran and Salah’) replied, ‘I started a Surah in Salah. I did not like to break the Salah after leaving the Surah incomplete. By Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ! If the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم had not given me the responsibility of keeping watch, I must have completed the Surah even by giving my life. Since the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم ordered me to keep a watch, it was my responsibility to carry it out in a proper way. When I found myself seriously injured, I cut my Salah short due to the same sense of responsibility and woke you up so that the enemy may not attack any more.

(‘Uyoon-ul-Hikayaat, part 1, pp. 33)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

سُـبْحٰـنَ الـلّٰــه عَزَّوَجَلَّ! May I be sacrificed for them! These blessed personalities had such a great devotion to Salah and Holy Quran that they did not care for their lives and remained busy with Salah. On the other hand, we do not offer Salah, and if we do, we are preoccupied with our home so much that we offer Salah extremely quickly, as though we offer it in haste. Remember! Due to offering Salah hastily, sometimes we make such mistakes during Salah which may invalidate our Salah.

Sayyiduna Huzayfah Bin Yamaan رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهُ saw a person who would not perform Ruku’ and Sujood completely during Salah. He رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهُ said to him: ‘If you die whilst offering Salah in the way you have just offered, you will not die in accordance with the teachings of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم .’

(Sahih Bukhari, vol. 1, pp. 284, Hadees 808)

It is also narrated that he رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهُ asked: ‘How long have you been offering Salah like this?’ The person replied: ‘For forty years.’ He رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهُ said: ‘You have not offered any Salah at all for forty years and if you die in the same state, you will not die on the religion of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم .’

(Sunan Nasa`ee, pp. 225, Hadees 1309)

اَلْاَمَان وَ الْـحَفِیْظ May Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ protect our faith!

اٰمِيۡن بِجَاهِ النَّبِيِّ الۡاَمِيۡن صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيۡهِ وَاٰلِه ٖ وَسَلَّم

صَلُّوۡ اعَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Teaching of offering Salah with calmness

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ has narrated that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم came to the Masjid; a person came and offered Salah and then went to the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and said Salam to him. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied to his Salam and said: ‘Go and offer Salah because you have not offered Salah [correctly].’ The man went back and offered Salah in the same way. He then came to the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and said Salam to him. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied to his Salam and said: ‘Go and offer Salah because you have not offered Salah.’ This happened thrice. The man humbly said: ‘By Him Who has sent you with the Truth! I know the same Salah. Please teach me.’ The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said: ‘When you stand for Salah say Takbeer and then recite as much from the Holy Quran as much you can recite easily and then perform Ruku calmly. Then raise your head from the Ruku’ and stand up straight, then perform Sajdah calmly, then raise your head and sit calmly and then complete the whole Salah in the same way.

(Sahih Bukhari, vol. 1, pp. 152, Hadees 757)

From this blessed Hadees it has been learnt that it is Wajib to repeat the Salah due to Wajib being missed. Remember, Sajdah Sahw becomes Wajib if

a Wajib is missed forgetfully during the Salah, and if a Wajib is missed deliberately, then repeating the Salah becomes Wajib.

Moreover, it has also been learnt that it is Wajib to offer Salah calmly because that elderly man came after offering Salah hastily, therefore he was asked to repeat his Salah; that is, every time when he would come after offering Salah, he would say Salam and would be asked to go back.

The Blessed and Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم did not teach him the method of Salah the very first time, but rather he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم taught him the method after making him offer Salah many times so that he could remember this event and he could memorize this ruling very well because the thing we get with great labour and wait, settles in the mind.

(Mirat-ul-Manajih, vol. 2, pp. 12)

Dear Islamic sisters! From the blessed Hadees and its commentary it has become obvious that we should offer Salah with extreme calmness without any haste. It has been stated in the blessed Hadees that: ‘Perform Ruku’ with calmness then stand up straight raising your head from Ruku’, then perform Sajdah with calmness, then sit calmly raising your head from sajdah’, this is called Ta’deel-e-Arkaan.

‘Allamah Maulana Mufti Muhammad Amjad ‘Ali A’zami رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has stated in Bahar-e-Shari’at: Maintaining Ta’deel-e-Arkaan, i.e. staying in Ruku’, Sujood, Qawmah [standing after Ruku’] and Jalsah [sitting between two Sajdahs] for the amount of time in which ‘ سُبْحٰنَ اﷲ ’ can be uttered at least once, is Wajib. Sajdah Sahw becomes Wajib if someone forgets Ta’deel-e-Arkaan.

(Bahar-e-Shari’at, vol. 1, pp. 518, Part. 4, pp. 711)

Anyhow, one should have information about the Wajibat, Makruhaat, Mufsidaat (The acts which invalidate Salah) of Salah, and other necessary rulings along with the correct method of performing Wudu and Ghusl (the ritual bath) so that our Salahs do not become invalid because the way offering Salah is obligatory for every adult and sane Muslim, similarly, it is also obligatory to learn rulings on Salah relevant to circumstances.

(Bahar-e-Shari’at, part 3, vol. 1, pp. 518; part 4, vol. 1, pp. 711)

Introduction of the book, ‘Salah for Islamic sisters’

اَلْـحَمْـدُ لـِلّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ! Shaykh-e-Tareeqat, Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, the founder of Dawat-e-Islami, ‘Allamah Maulana Abu Bilal Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qaadiri Razavi Ziyaee دَامَـتْ بَـرَكَـاتُـهُـمُ الْـعَـالِـيَـه out of his enthusiasm for serving Islamic sisters, has compiled an extremely easy book namely, ‘ Salah For Islamic sisters ’, which contains rulings regarding Salah. This book consists of necessary rulings relating to Islamic sisters. Therefore, every Islamic sister should buy and study this important book and also give this book as gift to other Islamic sisters. اِنْ شَــآءَالـلّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ you will attain abundance blessings.

Dear Islamic sisters, alas! At present, many efforts are made to gain knowledge of worldly arts and sciences and for this purpose great sacrifice of wealth and time is made. On the other hand, no one makes any effort to learn the rulings on Salah which is a pillar of Deen [Islam]. It is quite possible that our Salahs may become invalid due to a lack of Islamic knowledge and haste and we are unaware of it. Our pious predecessors and saints of Islam رَحِمَهُمُ الـلّٰـهُ تَـعَالٰی had a deep attachment to Salah. Taking care of all the manners of Salah these personalities not only would offer all obligatory Salahs, but they would also offer Nafl Salah with extreme calmness. Let’s listen to three parables of pious saints of Islam about it for persuasion.

Salah of A’la Hadrat

Maulana Muhammad Husayn Chishti Nizami Fakhri رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه, who wrote Fatawa for A’la Hadrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه for few years, has said: A’la Hadrat رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه certainly offered Salah throughout his life with Jama’at; no matter how hot [temperature] it was he would always offer Salah wearing ‘Imamah and Angharkhay [a garment that is worn over Kurta] (i.e. he would make special arrangement even in clothing for Salah]. He رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه never offered especially obligatory Salah wearing only Topi and a normal Kurta. Maulana Muhammad Husayn Chishti Nizami Fakhri رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه further stated: The amount of care A’la Hadrat رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه would take while offering Salah is not seen these days. I always offer two Rak’at in the amount of time in which A’la Hadrat would offer one Rak’at. Other people would offer at

least 6 Rak’at or rather 8 Rak’at in the amount of time in which I offer 4 Rak’at. (Hayat-e-A’la Hadrat, vol. 1, pp. 153)

Where is that wood?

Sayyiduna Abul Ahwas رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has stated that a lady neighbour of Sayyiduna Mansoor Bin Mu’tamir رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه asked her father: ‘O dear father! There was a piece of wood on the roof of [the house of] Sayyiduna Mansoor Bin Mu’tamir رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه, where is it?’ He replied: ‘O daughter! It was [not a piece of wood, but rather] Sayyiduna Mansoor رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه who used to offer Salah in the night. (Hilyat-ul-Awliya, pp. 46, Raqm 6269)

Sayyiduna ‘Ala Bin Saalim ‘Abadi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has stated about him that Sayyiduna Mansoor Bin Mu’tamir رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه would offer Salah on his [house’s] roof. When he رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه passed away, a boy asked his mother: Dear mother! There was a date trunk on the roof of so-and-so; where is it now? The mother replied: O son! It was not a trunk, but rather Sayyiduna Mansoor رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه, who [would worship, and now] has passed away.

(Hilyat-ul-Awliya, pp. 46, Raqm 6260)

Salah of Sayyiduna Haatim Asam رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه

Sayyiduna Haatim Asam رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه once went to meet Sayyiduna ‘Aasim Bin Yusuf Muhaddis رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه . Sayyiduna ‘Aasim Bin Yusuf رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه asked him: ‘O Haatim! Do you offer Salah properly?’ He رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه replied in the affirmative, then Sayyiduna ‘Aasim رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه asked: Tell me. How do you offer Salah? Sayyiduna Haatim رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه replied: When the time for Salah comes near, I perform my Wudu completely in an extremely proper manner.

When the time of Salah comes I step on the prayer mat and stand in such a manner that each joint of my body properly adjusts to its place, I then imagine that the Holy Ka’bah is between both of my eyebrows and Maqaam-e- Ibraheem is in front of my chest. Then having this belief in my heart that Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ is aware of my apparent condition and all hidden thoughts of my heart; I stand in a way as if my feet are on Siraat Bridge and Paradise to my right, and Hell to my left, and Malak-ul-Maut [the angel of death] behind me,

and I feel as if this Salah is the last Salah of my life. After this I recite Takbeer-e- Tahrimah with extreme sincerity, and then recite Holy Quran with extreme concentration and contemplation. I then perform Ruku’ and Sujood with utmost humbleness and in a beseeching manner and in the same way I offer the entire Salah with extreme humility and the presence of the heart.

Having heard this Sayyiduna ‘Aasim رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه asked surprisingly: ‘O Haatim! Do you really offer Salah always and every time in the same manner?’ Sayyiduna Haatim رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه replied: ‘Yes! I have been offering every Salah of mine always and every time in the same manner for thirty years’. (Ruh-ul-Bayan, vol. 1, pp. 33)

اَوقات کے اندر ہی پڑھوں ساری نَمازیں

الله! عبادت میں مِرے دل کو لگا دے

Dear Islamic sisters! Have you noticed how our pious predecessors رَحِمَهُمُ الـلّٰـهُ تَـعَالٰی used to offer long Salahs with extreme humility and the presence of the heart and mind, great yearning and concentration! On the other hand, if we are privileged to offer Salah, we offer it with extreme laziness and carelessness by looking here and there due to which we do not attain extreme humility and presence of the heart from the beginning.

Where should our sight be during Salah?

A’la Hadrat’s caliph Sayyid Ayyub ‘Ali Razavi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has stated: (One day), after Zuhr Salah, A’la Hadrat Imam Ahmed Raza Khan رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه was reciting Wazifah in the Masjid, a stranger came in front of him and started offering Salah. While performing Ruku’, raising his neck, he kept looking at the place of Sajdah. After he had completed his Salah, A’la Hadrat رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه called him and asked: Why did you raise your neck so much in Ruku’? He replied that he had been looking at the place of Sajdah. A’la Hadrat رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه said: The sight should not be focused at the place of Sajdah in every state. He then said: In the state of Qiyam, the sight should be at the place of Sajdah; in the state of Ruku’, it should be at the toes; in the state of Tasmee’ (standing after Ruku’), it should be on the chest; in the state of Sajdah it should be on nose and in

the state of Qu’ood (when reciting Attahiyyat etc.) it should be on the lap. Moreover, while performing Salam, the sight should be at the shoulders keeping in mind Katibeen (angels who record our deeds).

(Hayat-e-A’la Hadrat, vol. 1, pp. 303)

Dear Islamic sisters! It has become obvious that we should avoid looking here and there during Salah. Remember! Madani pearls about sleeping and waking up

Let’s listen to the Sunan and manners of sleeping and awakening from the booklet “ 101 Madani Pearls ” written by Shaykh-e-Tareeqat, Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, the founder of Dawat-e-Islami, ‘Allamah Maulana Abu Bilal Muhammad Ilyas Attari Qaadiri Razavi دَامَـتْ بَـرَكَـاتُـهُـمُ الْـعَـالِـيَـه:

1 Dust the bed off properly before sleeping, so that poisonous insects etc., if any, are removed.

2 Recite this Du’a before sleeping:

اَللّٰھُمَّ بِاسْمِكَ اَمُوْتُ وَ اَحْیٰ

O Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, I die and live (sleep and wake up) by Your name.

(Sahih Bukhari, vol. 4, pp. 196, Hadees 6325)

3 Do not sleep after ‘Asr, as there is a fear of losing the intellect. The Noble Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said, ‘Whoever sleeps after ‘Asr and loses his intellect, should blame only himself.’

(Musnad Abi Ya’la, vol. 4, pp. 278, Hadees 4897)

4 It is Mustahab [preferable] to nap for a while in the afternoon.

(Fatawa Hindiyah,, vol. 5, pp. 376)

A legendary and leading scholar of Shari’ah and Tareeqah, ‘Allamah Maulana Mufti Muhammad Amjad ‘Ali A’zami رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه says, ‘This is probably for those who remain awake at night offering S alah, doing Zikr of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, or reading and studying religious books, so that the tiredness caused by staying awake at night can be removed by the midday nap.’ (Bahar-e-Shari’at, vol. 16, pp. 79)

5 It is Makruh [disliked] to sleep in the beginning of the day or between Maghrib and ‘Isha. (Fatawa Hindiyyah, vol. 5, pp. 376)

6 To sleep in the state of purity is Mustahab.

7 First sleep on your right side facing Qiblah with your right hand under the right cheek for some duration, and then on your left side.

(Fatawa Hindiyyah, vol. 5, pp. 376)

8 Remember the grave as you go to sleep, as in the grave we will be alone and there will be no one but our deeds.

9 When going to sleep, busy yourself with the remembrance of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ . Keep reciting Tahleel, Tasbih and Tahmeed (i.e. keep reciting

(لَاۤ اِلٰہَ اِلَّا اللّٰہُ), (سُبْحٰنَ اللّٰہِ) and (اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰہِ)) until you fall asleep because a person wakes up in the same state that he falls asleep in, and on the Day of Judgement a person will rise in the same state that he dies in.

(Fatawa Hindiyyah, vol. 5, pp. 376)

10 Recite this Du’a upon waking up:

اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰہِ الَّذِیْۤ اَحْیَانَا بَعْدَ مَاۤ اَمَاتَنَا وَ اِلَیْہِ النُّشُوْرُ

All praise is to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ who gave us life after death and to Him we will return. (Sahih Bukhari, vol. 4, pp. 196, Hadees 6325)

Make intention that we will give all our Sadaqat-e-Wajibah and Naafilah (i.e. Zakah, Sadaqat, donations etc.) to Dawat-e-Islami, the Madani movement of devotees of Rasool, اِنْ شَــآءَال ـ لّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ . May Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ enable us to act upon it.

اٰمِــيْن بِـجَـاهِ الـنَّـبِـیِّ الْاَمِــيْن صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم

All Islamic sisters are requested to purchase the pamphlet “ Horrific Grave Scene of the One Indulging in Sins during Ramadan ” from Maktaba-tul-Madinah in a large quantity or at least 12 or as much as one can afford and distribute them and affix it at a prominent place at your home. اِنْ شَــآءَال ـ لّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ by its blessings the mind-set of avoiding sins would develop.
