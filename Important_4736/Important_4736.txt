What intentions should be made before Wudu?

Dear Islamic sisters! After listening to the medical benefits of Wudu it is hoped that we must have made our mind to be in the state of Wudu, but remember, the science of medicine is not certain. Scientific researches are not even final and they also keep changing. However, the commandments of Allah Almighty and His Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم are final and certain

and they will never change. We should act upon Sunnahs not to attain medical benefits, but to only please Allah Almighty. Those who perform Wudu so that their blood pressure becomes normal or they get refreshed or fast for dieting so that they can get the benefits of appetite, travel to Madinah so that they can have some change of climate and get some relief from their domestic chores or study Islamic books to pass time, will not get reward for their deeds because of such intentions. If we perform deeds to please Allah Almighty, we will also get reward along with their benefits. Therefore keeping in view apparent and hidden etiquette we should also perform Wudu to please Allah Almighty.

صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب

Dear Islamic sisters! Though we succeed in cleansing our outer-self by virtue of the blessing of Wudu, more than this we desperately need to cleanse our hearts of the filth of sins. Therefore the way we purify our outer-self of sins by performing Wudu, in the same way, when we cleanse our inner-self by making repentance and bow down before our Lord we will get more blessings.

Great Madani method of Tasawwuf

On page 425 of ‘Ihya-ul-‘Uloom’, volume one published by Maktaba-tul-Madinah the publishing department of Dawat-e-Islami, highlighting the importance of the cleanliness of the heart, Sayyiduna Imam Muhammad Ghazali رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: When we finish Wudu and pay attention to Salah, we should have this thing in mind that I have cleansed my outer-self, therefore I should feel ashamed of making Munajat in Divine court without cleansing my heart because Allah Almighty sees it and it is certain that the heart can be cleansed by repentance. It is necessary for the heart to like good manners and dislike bad manners. So the person who is confined to only outer purity is like the one who intends to invite the king to his home and in order to please the king, he cleans and paints the outside of his home but leaves the interior dirty and full of garbage. Of course, the king will be very

displeased with such a person. Allah Almighty knows better. (Ihya-ul-‘Uloom, vol. 1, pp. 185)

Madani pearls of avoiding wastage of water

Dear Islamic sisters! Let’s have the privilege to listen to a few Madani pearls of avoiding wastage of water. First of all listen to two blessed sayings of Mustafa صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم:

1. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said: There is no goodness in making plenty of water flow during Wudu and this deed is from the devil. (Kanz-ul-‘Ummal, vol. 9, pp. 144, Hadees 26255)

2. Once the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw a person performing Wudu, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said: Do not waste, do not waste.

(Ibn-e-Majah, vol. 1, pp. 254, Hadees 424)

3. If someone performs Wudu with Waqf water, so using more water than needed is Haraam in the sight of all Islamic scholars.

(Wudu ka Tareeqah, pp. 42)

4. Some people fill water in their hands in a way that water spills. The spilled water is wasted. Care must be taken in this regard.

5. Repent of all past unlawful wastage and make every possible attempt to avoid it in future.

6. During Wudu, open the tap carefully. During Wudu, keep one hand on the tap wherever possible and keep turning the tap off when the water is not needed.

7. Make a habit of turning your tap off completely so that not a single drop of water leaks while using a Miswak, rinsing, gargling, cleaning your nose, doing Khilal of your hands, feet; and wiping your head.

8. Avoid draining cold water for using hot water for Wudu, Ghusl, washing clothes, utensils etc. Pay attention to it especially in winter. You can avoid wasting this cold water by collecting it in some sort of container (for later use).

9. Also use water carefully when forming soap lather (rubbing your hands) in order to clean your hands or face.

10. After use, place your soap in a totally water free soap holder.

11. If you have a constantly leaking tap, you must take immediate action to get it repaired; otherwise water will continue to go to waste.

12. Be very careful when eating, drinking tea or other beverages, cutting fruits etc. so that each and every particle of food and drop of beverage is used.
