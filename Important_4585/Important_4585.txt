اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰہِ رَبِّ الْعٰلَمِیْنَ وَ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلٰی سَیِّدِ الْمُرْسَلِیْنَ ط

اَمَّا بَعْدُ فَاَعُوْذُ بِاللّٰہِ مِنَ الشَّیْطٰنِ الرَّجِیْمِ ط بِسْمِ اللہِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّ حِیْم ط

Mi’raaj Journey and Ghayb-knowledge of Mustafa ﷺ

اَلصَّلٰوۃُ وَ السَّلَامُ عَلَیْكَ یَا رَسُولَ اللہ وَعَلٰی اٰلِكَ وَ اَصْحٰبِكَ یَا حَبِیْبَ اللہ

اَلصَّلٰوۃُ وَ السَّلَامُ عَلَیْكَ یَا نَبِیَّ اللہ وَعَلٰی اٰلِكَ وَ اَصْحٰبِكَ یَا نُوْرَ اللہ

Please note that this speech was originally written for Islamic brothers, we have done necessary changes for Islamic sisters. It may be possible that some words could have been remained unchanged! If so, Muballighah should change the wordings accordingly at the time of delivering this speech. (Majlis-e-Tarajim)

Excellence of reciting Durood

The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said: ‘ حَيْثُ مَا كُنْتُمْ فَصَلُّوْا عَلَيَّ فَاِنَّ صَلَاتَكُمْ تَبْلُغُنِيْ ’ i.e. Wherever you are, recite Durood upon me because your Durood reaches me. (Al-Mu’jam-ul-Kabeer, vol. 3, pp. 82, Raqm, 2729)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Dear Islamic sisters! Let’s make good intentions for attaining the pleasure of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and earning rewards. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said, ‘ نِيَّةُ الۡمُؤۡمِنِ خَيۡرٌ مِّنۡ عَمَلِه ٖ ’ The intention of a believer is better than his action .

(Al-Mu’jam-ul-Kabeer, vol. 6, pp. 185, Hadees 5942)

Madani pearl

The more righteous intentions one makes the greater reward she will attain.

NOTE: ​ The intentions mentioned below can be modified as per situation

Intentions of listening to the Bayan

1. Lowering my eyes, I will listen to the Bayan attentively.

2. Instead of resting against a wall etc., I will sit in reverential posture like that in Tashahhud as long as possible with the intention of paying respect to religious discourse.

3. I will make room for other Islamic sisters by folding my hands and limbs and by moving slightly.

4. If someone pushes me, I will remain patient and calm and avoid staring, snapping, and arguing with them.

5. When I hear صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب, اُذۡكُرُوا اللّٰه, تُوۡبُوۡا اِلَي اللّٰه, etc., I will reply in low voice with the intention of gaining reward and encouraging others to also recite.

6. After Ijtima, I will take the lead to say Salam and shake hands and make individual effort.

7. During the speech, I will avoid the unnecessary use of mobile phone.

8. Neither will I record the speech, nor any kind of voice as it is not permitted.

9. Whatever I listen I will act upon it and later on convey it to others. In this way, I will be privileged to propagate the call towards righteousness.

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Dear Islamic sisters! Let’s have the privilege of listening to a faith-refreshing parable of Mi’raaj in order to attain the blessing from the blessed Zikr of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم .

Brief parable of Mi’raaj

On page 13 to 22 of Faizan-e-Mi’raaj, a book published by Maktaba-tul-Madinah the publishing house of Dawat-e-Islami, it has been written: It was the beautiful and luminous night of Monday, two years before Hijrah, on the eleventh year of Prophethood, on 27 th Rajab-ul-Murajjab. [1] And, after offering ‘Isha Salah the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was resting at the home of Sayyidatuna Umm-e-Haani رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا, his cousin. [2] The blessed roof of the blessed home opened, Sayyiduna Jibra`eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام came and brought the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم from the home of Sayyidatuna Umm-e-Haani رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا to Masjid-ul-Haraam and made him lie at the Hateem of the Ka’bah. (Fath-ul-Baari, vol. 7, pp. 256, Taht-al-Hadees 3887)

Expanding the chest

Dear Islamic sisters! When the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was lying on his side in the Hateem of the Ka’bah and still feeling drowsy [3], Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام came. This time he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام split the blessed chest from the collarbone to the body-part below the blessed belly and took out the blessed heart. Then a golden tray full of Iman and Hikmah [faith and wisdom] was brought. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام washed the blessed heart with Zamzam water and then filled it with Iman and Hikmah and then kept it at its place. (Sahih Bukhari, pp. 976, Hadees 3887)

Ride on Buraaq

Afterwards, a white animal bigger than a donkey and smaller than a mule, called Buraaq was brought to the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم for riding on. [4] It was already saddled and bridled. [5] Its speed was so amazing that it would put its step as far as the eye could see. [6] When climbing to a height, its

front legs would become short and its back legs would become long; and when climbing down, its front legs would become long and back legs would become short. For this reason, in both cases, its back would remain even [1] and the blessed rider would face no trouble. (Sharh Zurqaani, vol. 8, pp. 75)

When the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم intended to get on it and went near it, it started jumping with delight. Having seen this, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام placed his hand on its mane [hair of its neck] and said: O Buraaq! Do you not feel shy? By Allah Almighty! No personage having more respect and miracles than the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has ever ridden on you. Having heard this, Buraaq felt ashamed, dripping with sweat. It stopped jumping and became calm. (Seerat-un-Nabawiyyah li Ibn Hishaam, vol. 2, pp. 35)

Departed for Bayt-ul-Muqaddas

The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم then got on the Buraaq and departed for Bayt-ul-Muqaddas with tremendous glory. Sayyiduna ‘Allamah Boosayri رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said:

سَرَیْتَ مِنْ حَرَمٍ لَیْلًا اِلٰی حَرَمٍ کَمَا سَرَی الْبَدْرُ فِیْ دَاجٍ مِّنَ الظُّلُمِ

(Qaseeda-tul-Burdah, pp. 237)

i.e. he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم travelled to Haram-e-Bayt-il-Maqdis [ بَیْتِ الْمَقْدِس ] from Haram-e-Ka’bah in a glorious way like the full moon that passes, spreading light in the very dark night.

During this luminous journey, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام, the chief of angels, was also accompanying him. (Sahih Bukhari, pp. 976, Hadees 3887)

Salah was offered at three places

During the journey, at one place, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly asked the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to offer Salah after getting off. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم offered Salah. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly said: Do

you know at which place you have offered Salah? You have offered Salah in Taybah (i.e. sacred Madinah). Migration will take place towards it. At another place, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly asked the Beloved and Blessed Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to offer Salah after getting off. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم offered Salah. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly said: Do you know at which place you have offered Salah? You have offered Salah at Toor-e-Seena where Allah Almighty blessed Sayyiduna Musa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام with Kalam. At another place, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly asked the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to offer Salah after getting off. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم offered Salah. Then Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly said: Do you know at which place you have offered Salah? You have offered Salah at Bayt-e-Lahm [ بَیْتِ لَحْم ] where Sayyiduna ‘Isa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام was born. (Sunan Nasa`ee, pp. 81, Hadees 448)

Some observations during journey to Bayt-ul-Muqaddas

Observing the wonders of Divine power, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was going to Bayt-ul-Muqaddas. On the way, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw an old woman standing. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم asked Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام: Who is she? He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly replied: Your Highness! Please continue to move forward. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم moved forward, then someone called him out and said: ‘ ہَلُمَّ یَا مُحَمَّد ’ O Muhammad (صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم)! Come here. But Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly said the same: Your Highness! Please continue to move. Without stopping, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم moved forward and then saw a group. Humbly saying Salam to the Holy Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم they said: ‘ اَلسَّلَامُ عَلَیْکَ یَا اَوَّلُ، اَلسَّلَامُ عَلَیْکَ یَا آخِرُ، اَلسَّلَامُ عَلَیْکَ یَا حَاشِرُ ’ i.e. O the first (صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم)! Peace be upon you; O the last (صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم)! Peace be upon you. O Haashir (صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم)! Peace be upon you. Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly said: Your Highness! Please give reply to their Salam. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied to their Salam. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم then passed by another group. The same happened there and then he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم passed by the third group and the same thing happened.

Later on, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly said to the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم: The old woman you saw while she was standing at a corner

of the path was the world. Only as much part of its life is left as that of this old woman is left. The one who wanted to attract you was Iblees (Satan) the enemy of Allah Almighty. He wanted you to get interested in him. And, those who humbly said Salam to you were Sayyiduna Ibraheem, Sayyiduna Musa and Sayyiduna ‘Isa عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام . (Dalaail-un-Nubuwwah lil-Bayhaqi, vol. 2, pp. 362)

Sayyiduna Musa in Salah

It is stated in Sahih Muslim: When the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم passed by the blessed grave of Sayyiduna Musa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام that is located near the red mound of sand, he [i.e. Sayyiduna Musa عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام ] was offering Salah, standing in his grave. (Sahih Muslim, pp. 927, Hadees 2375)

Arrival in Bayt-ul-Muqaddas

Observing the wonders of Divine powers and meeting blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم reached the sacred city in which Masjid-ul-Aqsa is situated. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم entered the city through its Bab-e-Yamani and then moved towards the Masjid. [1] He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم tied the Buraaq to that ring of the door of the Masjid to which blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام would tie their animals for riding. Later on, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام took Buraaq to the [premises] of the Masjid. He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام made a hole into a stone through his finger and then tied the Buraaq to it.

(Sharh Zurqaani, vol. 8, pp. 103)

Leading Salah offered by blessed Ambiya

In order to show the eminence of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم all blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام were gathered in Bayt-ul-Muqaddas. [2] When he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم went there, seeing him all these blessed personages welcomed him. At the time of Salah, they all requested him to lead the Salah. Then holding his blessed hand, Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام brought him ahead. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم led the Salah offered by all blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام .

(Al-Mu’jam-ul-Awsat, vol. 3, pp. 65, Hadees 3879; Seerah Halbiyah, vol. 1, pp. 525)

Sayyiduna ‘Allamah Boosayri رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said:

وَقَدَّمَتْکَ جَمِیْعُ الْاَنْبِیَاءِ بِہَا وَالرُّسُلِ تَقْدِیْمَ مَخْدُوْمٍ عَلٰی خَدَمِ

(Qaseeda-tul-Burdah, pp. 240)

i.e. as a master is ahead of his servants, all blessed Ambiya and Rusul in Bayt-ul-Muqaddas requested the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to be ahead of them.

سُـبْحٰـنَ الـلّٰــه! What a beautiful Salah! All the Ambiya and Rusul عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام are Muqtadis, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is the Imam and the first Qiblah is the place of Salah. Indeed, no such Salah was offered in the universe before; the sky never saw such a beautiful scene. Anyhow, today the secret of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم being the first and the last has also been revealed and the meaning has become as clear as the day because today the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم, who is the last Nabi, is leading the Salah of the previous Ambiya and Rusul عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام .

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Meetings with blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام

When the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم went out of Bayt-ul-Muqaddas, Sayyiduna Jibra`eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام presented two bowls—one of wine and another of milk. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم picked up the bowl of milk. Having seen this, Sayyiduna Jibra`eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام said: You have chosen nature. If you had picked up the bowl of wine, your Ummah would have gone astray. Then Sayyiduna Jibra`eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام took the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to the sky. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم met Sayyiduna Aadam عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام in the first sky, Sayyiduna Yahya and Sayyiduna ‘Isa عَـلَيْـهِمَا الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام in the second sky and also had some conversation with them. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم met Sayyiduna Yusuf عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام in the third sky, Sayyiduna Idrees عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام in the fourth sky and Sayyiduna Haroon عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام in the fifth sky. back against Bayt-ul-Ma’moor (Qiblah of angels) where 70,000 angels enter every day. Every Nabi welcomed the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم by saying ‘Welcome! O righteous Rasool’. Then he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was made to do a tour of Paradise. It has been stated in the Holy Quran in these words: In Ayah 8 and 9 of Surah An-Najm, part 27 Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has stated:

ثُمَّ دَنَا فَتَدَلّٰىۙ(۸) فَكَانَ قَابَ قَوْسَیْنِ اَوْ اَدْنٰىۚ(۹)

Translation from Kanz-ul-Iman: Then the Spectacle became closer, and came down in full view. So the distance between the Spectacle and the beloved was only two arms’ length, or even less.

Afterwards what happened there is beyond our intellect. Having beheld Allah Almighty, made a wonderful tour and seen clear signs from Allah Almighty, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم came back to the earth and entered Bayt-ul-Muqaddas and mounted Buraaq to set out for Makkah Mukarramah. After passing through all these stages, when he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم reached Masjid-ul-Haraam, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم slept because much part of the night was still left. (Seerat-e-Mustafa, pp. 735; summarised)

Dear Islamic sisters! When the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم related the parable of Mi’raaj [the Ascension] to the Quraysh, they were quite astonished and started asking questions in order to test him so that they could prove him a liar, مَـعَـاذَ الـلّٰـه .

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: The questions were also useless. For instance, how many pillars does Bayt-ul-Muqaddas have? How many stairs are there? Where is the Mimber [pulpit] located? Obviously, these things cannot be remembered even if one sees them again and again; so how can a person remember if he sees only once. A few people from Quraysh said: We do not know about ‘Arsh and Kursi which you are talking about, but we have seen Bayt-ul-Muqaddas. Tell us the signs of Bayt-ul-Muqaddas. [1] (So when the things related to Bayt-ul-Muqaddas were asked), the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم became a little uncertain because he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم did enter Bayt-ul-Muqaddas but did not observe the things in it minutely. Furthermore, it was a very dark night.

On that occasion, Allah Almighty commanded Sayyiduna Jibra`eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام to lift Bayt-ul-Muqaddas on his wings and place it in Makkah Mukarramah near the house of Sayyiduna ‘Aqeel رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ . Sayyiduna Jibra’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام did so. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم would answer to their questions after seeing it. (Remember that) Lifting Bayt-ul-Muqaddas and bringing it to the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is the miracle of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . Throne of Bilqees (was also brought to the court of Sayyiduna Sulayman عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام after being lifted) and it was the miracle of Sayyiduna Sulayman عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام . (Seerat Sayyid-ul-Ambiya, pp. 131)

Dear Islamic sisters! Have you heard how elevated and great is the glory of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! Allah Almighty blessed him with the ride of the Heavenly-Buraaq in order to show him great Divine signs in the shortest period of time on the 27 th night of Rajab-ul-Murajjab. Allah Almighty called His Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to Masjid-ul-Aqsa from Masjid-ul-Haraam and from there to all the skies and showed the glory and excellence of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to different Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام and

angels. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was given the gift of Salahs and above all, he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ was blessed with beholding Allah Almighty and Allah Almighty also had Kalam with His Beloved directly.

Let’s listen to a blessed Hadees that tells us about the privilege our Beloved and Blessed Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم had of beholding Allah Almighty and having Kalam with Him.

I recognized everything

Sayyiduna Mu’aaz Bin Jabal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ has narrated: The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said: I saw my Rab. He kept His Dast-e-Qudrat between my shoulders; I felt its coolness in my chest; at the same time everything became bright for me and I recognized everything. (Sunan-ut-Tirmizi, vol. 5, pp. 160, Hadees 3246)

The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said: I saw my Rab in my best appearance. (i.e., that time my appearance was very good, as it is said that I met the ruler in a good dress, that is, I was wearing a good dress at the time of meeting, otherwise, Allah Almighty is free of Soorat [appearance]. Remember, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم came to us in Bashari (human) form and he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم met Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ in Noori form. A person wears a different dress at home and a different dress in the court and (in above mentioned Hadees) it is perhaps the Zikr of Mi’raaj event. Some have said that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw Allah Almighty in his dream, but the first one is very much true. For this reason, Deedar-e-Ilahi [the act of beholding Allah Almighty] has been proven. The factual truth is that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw Allah Almighty with his own blessed eyes. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said that Allah Almighty asked (me): Over what thing do Muqarrab (high-ranking) angels quarrel? (i.e. what are the deeds angels quarrel over regarding taking and presenting them to Allah Almighty - one says I will take it the other one says I will do so.) I humbly said: O Allah Almighty! You only know it. Then Allah Almighty placed

His Dast-e-Qudrat between my both shoulders, the coolness of which I felt into my chest, so whatever is in the skies and beneath the earth I have known all of them.) (Sunan Daarimi, vol. 2, pp. 170; Mirat-ul-Manajih, vol. 1, pp. 446)

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan Na’eemi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: This Hadees is the clear evidence of the vast knowledge of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . Allah Almighty blessed the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم with the knowledge of all seven skies, rather all the things above the skies and all seven earths and even each and every particle and drop beneath them.

Remember! Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ blessed His Beloved with the knowledge of everything —the past, present and future—things which will happen until the Judgement Day because deeds of people on the earth and quarrels of angels for these deeds in the sky will continue until the Judgement Day and today the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is watching them with his blessed eyes. (Mirat-ul-Manajih, vol. 1, pp. 446; summarised)

A’la Hadrat, Imam of Ahl-us-Sunnah, Maulana Shah Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has bestowed the thorough knowledge of everything from the beginning of the universe to the last day, it means whatever has happened, is happening and will happen, the detailed knowledge of each and every particle has been imparted by Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ to His Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم also possesses the knowledge of even the particle or grain of sand lying in extreme darkness. Not only does he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم have the knowledge, but he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is also watching the entire world and whatever will happen in it until the Judgement Day, like watching his own palm. Not a single particle either in the skies or beneath the earths is concealed from his blessed eyes.

Rather, whatever has been discussed here is like a small canal out of his vast oceans of knowledge. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم recognizes his entire Ummah much more than the person who knows those who spend time with him. Not only does he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم recognize his Ummah but he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is also watching their each and every deed and movement. He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is also aware of the thoughts that cross our minds. Further, all the oceans of

his knowledge and the knowledge of entire creation altogether have even no such comparison with the Divine knowledge which a little drop of water has with billions of oceans. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, vol. 15, pp. 74; summarised)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Dear Islamic sisters! When our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم answered to all the questions asked by a few people of Makkah about Bayt-ul-Muqaddas, so instead of believing in this great miracle of the Holy Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم they started testing it through their intellect, or rather they became busy proving this great miracle false. Therefore despite being disgraced upon asking about the signs of Bayt-ul-Muqaddas they did not stop due to their stubbornness and started asking questions about the caravan which went to Syria for trade from Makkah Mukarramah in order to test the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم .

The unbelievers of Quraysh said: You tell us about our caravans of traders if you have found them on the way. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied: I passed by a caravan of the tribe in a place called Roha [ روحاء ]. They were looking for their camel which they lost. I came towards their luggage which was lying there but found no one. A bowl of water was lying there which I drank and then covered it.

(Then the Ghayb-knowing Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said) Now the caravan will reach here on Wednesday at sunset. You may then ask them whether they found their water-filled bowl empty or not when they came back after finding their lost camel. Also ask them: While you were searching for the camel, did anyone say by calling you out that your camel is at such and such a place and upon which you were surprisingly saying: How have we heard the voice of Muhammad (صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم) in Syria? But when you looked for the camel in that very place as per the voice, did you find your camel or not? Quraysh said: Yes! It is alright as it is a big sign.

The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم then said: I also passed by a caravan of such and such a tribe whose two men were sitting on a camel. Frightened of

the speed of Buraaq, their camel ran away causing both of them to fall from the camel due to which one of them broke his wrist. Now that caravan will reach here exactly at noon on Wednesday. You may ask both of them about it. The unbelievers of Quraysh said: Very good; it is also a good sign.

The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم then said: I also passed by a caravan of a tribe through the place called Tan’eem [ تَنْعِیْم ]. In this caravan, a khaki-coloured camel was in the front carrying two striped bags of grain and a dark-skinned man was also sitting on it. In the same caravan, such and such a person was feeling cold and was asking for a blanket from his slave. This caravan has reached very near and will reach here at sunrise in the morning.

Before the sunrise, some people sat on a hill and started waiting for the caravan. Some people were assigned the task to wait for the sunrise so that they could pay special attention to it. Suddenly one of them shouted: Listen, look over there! The sun has risen. Meanwhile, someone said: Look over there, the caravan has also arrived. When people looked, they exactly saw a khaki-coloured camel was in front of the caravan carrying two striped sacks of grain.

Afterwards, at noon, on Wednesday a group of Quraysh sat on the hill and started waiting for the arrival of the caravan about whose arrival the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم had informed that it would reach at noon on Wednesday. Exactly at noon the second caravan also reached and the person whose news of falling from the camel was given had a broken wrist.

Thereafter, some people started waiting for the third caravan whose arrival was mentioned at sunset. The time of sunset came closer, but the caravan did not arrive. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم made Du’a to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ; the Du’a was heard and the sun was stopped. As the caravan reached, the sun also set. When the people of the caravan were asked about the things he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم mentioned, they testified about all the things. The way the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم mentioned, exactly in the same way things happened. Due to the disclosure of the miracles of the Beloved

Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم, the unbelievers were ashamed, but they did not embrace Islam. [1]

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Dear Islamic sisters! From the above-mentioned parable of Mi’raaj, we have perceived many Madani pearls. One of them is that Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has blessed His Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم with the great wealth of knowledge of Ghayb [unseen].

On returning from the journey of Mi’raaj, some people of Makkah, to test him, asked about Bayt-ul-Muqaddas and the caravans travelling for the purpose of trade so he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم by the bestowal of Allah Almighty splendidly gave correct answers to all their questions, even he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم mentioned the correct arrival timings of their trading caravans . The act of going to Bayt-ul-Muqaddas had nothing to do with the return of the caravans, that is, if the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم had not mentioned the timings of the arrival of the caravans, even then due to this, objection could not have been raised to his act of going to Bayt-ul-Muqaddas. But may I be sacrificed for the Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم also mentioned very clearly the things which had even nothing to do with the doubts of those who objected. It is a very strong evidence for his knowledge of Ghayb. It is a fact that cannot be denied because the blessed position of a Nabi is to tell the news of Ghayb.

In Bahar-e-Shari’at, ‘Allamah Maulana Mufti Muhammad Amjad ‘Ali A’zami رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has stated: Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام come to tell the news of Ghayb [unseen]. Paradise, Hell, Judgement Day, torment and reward are all Ghayb and nothing else. It is their blessed position to tell about the things where human intellect and senses cannot reach and it is called Ghayb. (Bahar-e-Shari’at, part 1, vol. 1, pp. 46)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

On page 17 of his booklet ‘ Siyah Faam Ghulam ’ [Dark-Skinned Slave], Shaykh-e- Tareeqat, Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, the founder of Dawat-e-Islami, ‘Allamah Maulana Abu Bilal Muhammad Ilyas ‘Attar Qaadiri Razavi Ziyaee دَامَـتْ بَـرَكَـاتُـهُـمُ الْـعَـالِـيَـه has said: By the bestowal of Allah Almighty, the Holy Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is also aware of the ages of his slaves as well as the things which are going to happen to them. There are many blessed Ayahs of the Holy Quran which give proof of the knowledge of Ghayb [unseen] of our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم . In Ayah 24 of Surah At-Takweer, part 30 Allah Almighty has stated:

وَ مَا هُوَ عَلَى الْغَیْبِ بِضَنِیْنٍۚ(۲۴)

Translation from Kanz-ul-Iman: And th is Nabi is not miserly upon the hidden.

(Part 30, Surah At-Takweer, Ayah 24)

And in Ayah 26 and 27 of Surah Al-Jinn, part 29 Allah Almighty has stated:

عٰلِمُ الْغَیْبِ فَلَا یُظْهِرُ عَلٰى غَیْبِهٖۤ اَحَدًاۙ(۲۶) اِلَّا مَنِ ارْتَضٰى مِنْ رَّسُوْلٍ

Translation from Kanz-ul-Iman: The All-Knowing of all the hidden does not give anyone the control over His secrets. Except to His chosen Noble Rusul.

(Part 29, Surah Al-Jinn, Ayah 26, 27)

‘Allamah Isma’eel Haqqi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said that blessed commentators have said: Allah Almighty blesses His specific Rusul with specific knowledge of Ghayb. Whatever knowledge of Ghayb blessed saints رَحِمَهُمُ الـلّٰـهُ تَـعَالٰی get, they get by the Wasilah [source] and blessings of blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام .

(Ruh-ul-Bayan, vol. 10, pp. 201)

Similarly, in Ayah 179 of Surah Aal-e-‘Imran, part 4, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has stated:

وَ مَا كَانَ اللّٰهُ لِیُطْلِعَكُمْ عَلَى الْغَیْبِ وَ لٰكِنَّ اللّٰهَ یَجْتَبِیْ مِنْ رُّسُلِهٖ مَنْ یَّشَآءُ ۪-

Translation from Kanz-ul-Iman: And it does not befit the glory of Allah to give the knowledge of Ghayb to you – i.e. the common people; but Allah does choose from His Rusul whomever He wills.

(Part 4, Surah Aal-e-‘Imran, Ayah 179)

Regarding this blessed Ayah ‘Allamah Maulana Sayyid Mufti Muhammad Na’eemuddin Muradabadi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has said: Allah Almighty blesses His highly respectable Rusul عَـلَـيْهِمُ السَّلَام with knowledge of Ghayb and the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is the best and the greatest Nabi amongst all Rusul. It has been proved by this blessed Ayah and many Ayahs and Ahadees that Allah Almighty bestowed the knowledge of Ghayb upon the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and the knowledge of Ghayb is his miracle.

Dear Islamic sisters! Our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم gave the news of Ghayb to his blessed companions رَضِىَ الـلّٰـهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُم many times by the knowledge Allah Almighty bestowed upon him.

Sound of footsteps of Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ

While touring Paradise, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم heard the sound of someone’s footsteps, regarding which, the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم was informed that he was Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ . (Mishkat-ul-Masabih, vol. 2, pp. 418, Hadees 6037)

May I be sacrificed for him! See the highly exalted and greatest glory of Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم heard the sound of his footsteps. How did Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ attain this great status? Let’s listen to it.

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ has narrated that (once) the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said to Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ at Fajr: O Bilal! Tell me which act you did in Islam for which you hope to receive the greatest reward because I heard the sound of your footsteps in front of me in Paradise. Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ humbly replied: [In Islam] I have not performed any such great act for which I hope to get benefit. However, when I performed

Wudu or Ghusl [ritual bath] anytime during the day or night I offered Salah as much as Allah Almighty wills me to do so. (Sahih Muslim, pp. 1025, Hadees 2458)

A great thinker of Ummah, ‘Allamah Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan Na’eemi رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه has also mentioned another meaning of the above-mentioned blessed Hadees that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw all these things physically in Mi’raaj, but he صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم asked this question some other day after Fajr Salah. He رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه further said: Sayyiduna Bilal’s entry into Paradise before the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم is like the entry of the servant who walks in front of his king.

Remember! During the night of Mi’raaj, neither did Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ accompany the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم to Paradise nor did he رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ was blessed with Mi’raaj, but rather the Holy Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم saw the event which will happen after the Judgement Day. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم will enter Paradise before [all] creation in a way that Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ will be in front of him as a servant.

We have learned some rulings from it. Firstly, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ made the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم aware of the consequences of people such as who will be sent to Paradise and who will be sent to Hell, what will be the level of the one who will be sent to Paradise and the one who will be sent to Hell. Secondly, the blessed ears of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم heard and the blessed eyes saw the events which will happen after hundreds of thousands of years. This event will happen after many hundreds of thousands of years after that date. But may I be sacrificed on the blessed ears which are listening to them today. Thirdly, the way a person lives his life in this world, in the same way he will be in the Hereafter, as Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ spent his life serving the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم in the world so he رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ will also be raised as a servant in the Hereafter. (Mirat-ul-Manajih, vol. 2, pp. 300)

O devotees of companions and Ahl-e-Bayt! Have you heard! By his great knowledge of Ghayb, our Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم heard the sound [that will be] produced by the feet of Sayyiduna Bilal رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ on the Judgement Day hundreds of thousands of years before.

Dear Islamic sisters! Surely it is a great blessing of Allah Almighty that He created us in the Ummah of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and also blessed us with the treasure of Iman [faith]. Therefore we all should keep making Du’a to Allah Almighty for steadfastness in Iman and death with Iman [faith]. Pondering over the protection of Iman [faith] we should keep ourselves away from all sins, especially from those evil acts which may destroy our faith.

It is stated in Sharh-us-Sudoor: Some Islamic scholars رَحِمَهُمُ الـلّٰـهُ تَـعَالٰی have said: There are four reasons for a bad end [death]. (1) Laziness in Salah (2) Drinking wine (3) Disobedience to parents (4) Causing inconvenience to Muslims.

(Sharh-us-Sudoor, pp. 27)

Remember! There are not only these four reasons for losing faith at the time of death, but rather many other reasons for having a bad end have been mentioned at different places. Madani pearls of clipping nails

O the devotees of Rasool! Let’s listen to some Madani pearls of clipping nails from the booklet ‘ 101 Madani Phool ’, written by Shaykh-e-Tareeqat, Ameer-e- Ahl-e-Sunnat دَامَـتْ بَـرَكَـاتُـهُـمُ الْـعَـالِـيَـه:

٭ It is Mustahab to clip nails on Friday . However, one should not wait for Friday if the nails have grown long. (Durr-e-Mukhtar, vol. 9, pp. 668)

٭ A legendary and leading scholar of Shari’ah and Tareeqah, ‘Allamah Maulana Mufti Amjad ‘Ali A’zami رَحْمَةُ الـلّٰـهِ تَـعَـالٰی عَـلَيْـه has said: It is narrated that whosoever clips his nails on Friday, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ will protect him from calamities till the next Friday and for three days thereafter, that is, for 10 days. (Mirqat-ul-Mafatih, vol. 8, pp. 212, Hadees 4422)

In one narration, it is stated that whosoever clips his nails on Friday, mercy will be showered [upon him] and [his] sins will be removed. (Durr-e-Mukhtar, Rad-dul-Muhtar, vol. 9, pp. 668)

٭ Following is summary of the narrated method of cutting nails: Begin with the index finger of the right hand and cut your nails in order up to the little finger; but do not cut your thumb nail. Now, beginning with the little finger of the left hand, cut your nails in order including that of the thumb. In the end, cut the nail of the right thumb. (Durr-e-Mukhtar, Rad-dul-Muhtar, vol. 9, pp. 670; Ihya-ul-‘Uloom, vol. 1, pp. 193)

٭ No specific order of cutting toenails has been narrated. It is better to start from the smallest toe on the right foot and cut in order, including the big toe, then cut the nails of left foot in order, beginning with the big toe including that of the small toe. (Durr-e-Mukhtar, vol. 9, pp. 670)

٭ It is Makruh to clip the nails in the state of Janabat, that is, when Ghusl has become Fard (obligatory). (Fatawa Hindiyyah, vol. 5, pp. 358)

٭ It is Makruh to bite nails with teeth. There is a fear of being inflicted with leprosy in doing so. (Fatawa Hindiyyah, vol. 5, pp. 358)

٭ Bury the nails after clipping them. (Fatawa Hindiyyah, vol. 5, pp. 358)
