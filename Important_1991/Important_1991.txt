I will be more than careful with the animal’s keeping, tying and slaughtering so as not to bother my neighbours and cause any inconvenience to passersby. (It is necessary to ensure that the walls of your neighbors are not stained with animal’s urine, dung or blood, etc.)

During the slaughter, I will not inflict brutal torture upon the animal. (For example, felling the animal on a stony ground, dragging it unnecessarily after felling it down, it is preferable to take the knife from the butchers soon after the slaughter, otherwise they usually crack the neck-bone of goats and if it is big animal, they remove skin from one side of the neck, stab knife into the chest and cut its heart veins instantly.

May Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ enable us to protect poor animals from cruelty – Ameen!)
