Excellence of reciting Salat-‘Alan-Nabi صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم

Sayyiduna ‘Abdullah Ibn ‘Amr Bin ‘Aas رَضِىَ الـلّٰـهُ تَـعَـالٰی عَـنْـهُمَا said, ‘Whoever recites Salat upon the Rasool of Rahmah, the Intercessor of Ummah صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم one time, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and His [blessed] angels will send mercies upon him seventy times.’ (Musnad Imam Ahmad Bin Hanbal, vol. 2, pp. 614, Hadees 6766)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Dear Islamic sisters! Before listening to the Bayan, let’s make good intentions for attaining rewards. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said, ‘ نِيَّةُ الۡمُؤۡمِنِ خَيۡرٌ مِّنۡ عَمَلِهٖ ’ The intention of a believer is better than his action .

(Al-Mu’jam-ul-Kabeer, vol. 6, pp. 185, Hadees 5942)

Two Madani pearls

-1 Without a good intention, no reward is granted for a good deed.

-2 The more righteous intentions one makes the greater reward she will attain.

Note: The intentions mentioned below can be modified as per situation

-1 Lowering my eyes, I will listen to the Bayan attentively.

-2 Instead of resting against a wall etc., I will sit in reverential posture like that in Tashahhud as long as possible with the intention of paying respect to the religious discourse.

-3 I will make room for other Islamic sisters by folding my hands and limbs and by moving slightly.

-4 If someone pushes me, I will remain patient and calm and avoid staring, snapping, and arguing with them.

-5 When I hear صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب, اُذۡكُرُوا اللّٰه, تُوۡبُوۡا اِلَي اللّٰه, etc., I will reply in low voice with the intention of gaining reward and encouraging others to also recite.

-6 After Ijtima, I will take the lead to say Salam and shake hands and make individual effort.

-7 During the speech, I will avoid the unnecessary use of mobile phone.

-8 Neither will I record the speech, nor any kind of voice as it is not permitted.

-9 Whatever I listen I will act upon it and later on convey it to others. In this way, I will be privileged to propagate the call towards righteousness.

Dear Islamic sisters! Allah Almighty has bestowed countless blessings upon us. The more we thank for these blessings, the less it is. One great blessing

out of these blessings is ‘children’. It is the responsibility of parents to provide education and upbringing in a proper manner to their children. How should parents provide their children education and upbringing so that they have good attributes?

Dear Islamic sisters! Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلٰى نَبِـيِّنَا وَعَـلَيْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام is the blessed and chosen Nabi of Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty has blessed him with special favours and honours, and that is why he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام always remained steadfast by Divine blessing in the Divine trials he faced. Let’s listen to a famous parable of Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام about his remaining pleased with the Will of Allah Almighty [the parable for commemorating which اَلْـحَمْـدُ لـِلّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ Muslims carry out Qurbani every year at Eid-ul-Adha (10, 11, 12 Zul-Hijjah) all over the world].

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Same dream during three nights

On the eighth night of Zul-Hajj, Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام dreamt that someone said, ‘Indeed Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has ordered you to slaughter your son.’ From dawn to dusk, he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام continued to contemplate whether the dream was from Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ or from Satan. This is the reason why the eighth of Zul-Hajj was named ‘ يَوۡمُ التَّرۡوِيَه ’, i.e. the day of contemplation. On the ninth night of Zul-Hajj, he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام had the same dream and was convinced that the dream was from Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, which is why the ninth of Zul-Hajj is referred to as ‘ يَوۡمِ عَرَفَه ’, i.e. the day of recognition. After he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام had the same dream once again on the tenth of Zul-Hajj, he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام made a firm intention of sacrificing his son, which is why the tenth of Zul-Hajj is called ‘ يَوۡمُ النَّحۡر ’, i.e. the day of slaughter. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, vol. 9, pp. 346)

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Unique manner of complying with Divine commandment

Dear Islamic sisters! The status of the blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام is higher and more exalted than the rest of the Creation, therefore the trials they face are also very severe, but may I be sacrificed on the patience and steadfastness of these blessed personalities who not only attain higher ranks in the blessed court of Allah Almighty by bearing these troubles and trials cheerfully, but they also keep themselves and their family members prepared all the time for the trials to come in the future. Such sacrifices of these people become guiding principles for the people until the world exists. Since the dreams of the blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام are Divine revelations.

(Al-Mustadrak, vol. 3, pp. 214, Tafseer Surah As-Saffaat, Hadees 3665)

Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام realized that Allah Almighty was commanding him to slaughter his son. He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام got immediately ready to sacrifice his beloved son on the Divine command and related the whole story to his young son, Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام and said: ‘It is the command of Allah Almighty that I should slaughter you. Now you tell me what your opinion is?’ It is stated in Tafseer-e-Khaazin that Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام did not consult Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام because he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام would act upon Sayyiduna Ismaeel’s decision if he did not give his consent, but rather it was aimed at testing Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام as to what his feelings were about the trial. It was also aimed to know about the patience and steadfastness of Sayyiduna Ismaeel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم in this trial and his obedience to the command of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ so that he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام could succeed in attaining the reward for obeying Divine commandment s . When Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام related this dream to Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام, the reply he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام gave has been mentioned in Ayah 102 of Surah Al-Saffaat, Part 23 in the following words:

قَالَ یٰۤاَبَتِ افْعَلْ مَا تُؤْمَرُ٘-سَتَجِدُنِیْۤ اِنْ شَآءَ اللّٰهُ مِنَ الصّٰبِرِیْنَ(۱۰۲)

Translation from Kanz-ul-Iman: He said, ‘O my father! Do what you are commanded to do! Allah willing, you will soon find me patiently enduring!’

(Part 23, Surah As-Saffaat, Ayah 102)

Tie me tightly with ropes

It is stated in Tafseer-e-Khaazin that Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام humbly said to his respected father: Dear father! Tie me tightly with ropes before you slaughter me so that I do not move lest my reward should get reduced. Also please protect your clothes from the splashes of my blood because my mother will get grieved if she sees it. Sharpen the knife so that it runs properly over my throat (i.e. my throat gets cut immediately) because death is extremely painful. Make me lie on my front placing my forehead over the ground so that you cannot see my face while slaughtering me. And when you go to my mother, please convey my Salam to her. And if you consider it appropriate, so please give my Qamee s [i.e. a long loose and full-sleeved shirt] to her. This will comfort her and help her have patience. Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام said, ‘O my son! How helpful you are to me in carrying out the order of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ!’

Thereafter, Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـصَّلٰوة ُ وَالـسَّلَام tied his son as suggested by him, made him lie on his front with his forehead placed over the ground, sharpened his knife, averted his eyes from his face and ran the knife over his throat but the knife did not cut, i.e. his throat was not slit. (Tafseer Khaazin, vol. 4, pp. 22)

Heavenly ram and collection of blessed words

When Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام made Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام lie on the ground for slaughtering him, Sayyiduna Jibraeel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام came with a ram from Paradise as Fidyah by the command of Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and said loudly from a distance ‘ اَللّٰهُ اَكۡبَرُ اَللّٰهُ اَكۡبَرُ ’. Having heard this voice, Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام raised his eyes towards the sky and realized that the time of the trial from Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has been over and a ram has been sent as Fidyah in place of [his] son. He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام, therefore, said delightedly ‘ لَآ اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَاللّٰهُ اَكۡبَرُ ’. When Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام heard this he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام said, ‘ اَللّٰهُ اَكۡبَرُ وَلِلّٰهِ الۡحَمۡدُ ’. Since then the Sunnah of reciting these blessed words by these three blessed personalities عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام has been going on and it will go on until the Day of Judgement. (Binayah Sharah Hidayah, vol. 3, pp. 130)

Dear Islamic sisters! The abovementioned blessed parable also contains Madani pearls for us. As you have heard when Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام related his dream to Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام, he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام instead of being sad and frightened, rejoiced, considering himself to be fortunate because Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ asked for his sacrifice and he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام got ready cheerfully to be sacrificed in the path of Allah Almighty. On the contrary, if somebody experiences a little difficulty or trouble in her life, she begins to complain. Being impatient she even مَـعَـاذَ الـلّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ sometimes utters words of disbelief and displeases Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ . On such an occasion, one should exercise great patience as this trial is also mercy from Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ . Sayyiduna Anas رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُ has narrated that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم stated, ‘Undoubtedly, more reward lies in a tough trial and when Allah Almighty loves any nation, He عَزَّوَجَلَّ makes them suffer a trial. So the one who is pleased with His Qaza, for him there is pleasure and the one who is displeased, for him there is displeasure.’ (Sunan Ibn Majah, Kitab-ul-Fitan, vol. 4, pp. 374, Raqm 4031)

This blessed event also gives us the lesson of giving upbringing to children. Our children are the apple of our eyes and peace of our hearts, but before this they are the bondmen of Allah Almighty, the Ummati of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and the important members of the Islamic society. If our upbringing does not make them worship Allah Almighty, obey the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم and love Sunnahs and hate sins, so we should not dream to make them our obedient because it is Islam that teaches a Muslim to become submissive and obedient to his parents. Therefore, we should always be ready to give our children moral and spiritual upbringing along with looking after their physical appearance, good food, nice clothing and other necessities.

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

How people started living in Makka-tul-Mukarramah

Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلٰى نَبِـيِّنَا وَعَـلَيْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام brought him [Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم ] and his mother, Sayyidatuna Haajirah رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا to Makkah and left them there. A long time passed and the Jurham tribe set up camp in Makkah

Mukarramah; they also started living there. (During the same period of time, Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام had also grown into a young man.) Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام married one of the women belonging to that tribe; his mother, Sayyidatuna Haajirah رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا passed away during this period of time. After a long time, Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام visited his son’s house and asked his wife, ‘Where is your husband?’ She humbly replied, ‘He has gone hunting; may Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ have mercy on you! Please have a seat, اِنْ شَــآءَالـلّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ he is about to come.’ Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام asked, ‘Do you have anything to eat?’ She replied in the affirmative and served him with milk and meat.’ When Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام asked her about their living, the wife of Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم humbly said: ‘We are well and اَلْـحَمْـدُ لـِلّٰـه عَزَّوَجَلَّ prosperous.’ Thereafter, Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام made Du’a of blessing for both of them and told her to convey his Salam when her husband would come, and asked her to tell him to continue to have his door frame.’

When Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام came, he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام felt the fragrance of his respected father and said to his wife: ‘Did anybody come here today?’ She replied, ‘Yes! A beautiful-faced and fragrant pious person came and then she related the whole event. She also said: ‘I washed his blessed head and these are his footsteps.’ Having heard the whole event, Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام said, ‘He (عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام) was my father, Sayyiduna Ibraheem and my door frame means you and he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم ordered me not to leave you.’

(Ruh-ul-Bayan, vol. 1, pp. 225)

Dear Islamic sisters! From this event, we have learnt the lesson that whenever a guest comes to us we should offer her hospitality as per our capacity. Instead of talking about our poverty, we should always thank Allah Almighty in every state. Giving upbringing to children, in particular, to daughters, parents should give them the Dars of fulfilling the rights of their husbands and avoiding ungratefulness. They should also give their daughters the Dars not to forget the favours they receive besides teaching them other manners because words of ungratefulness not only destroy worldly life of a woman, but they can also destroy her Hereafter.

The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has stated, ‘I saw women in majority in Hell.’ The blessed Companions رَضِىَ الـلّٰـهُ تَعَالٰی عَـنْهُم humbly asked: ‘Ya Rasool Allah صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم! What is the reason for this?’ He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied, ‘Because they are ungrateful.’ It was humbly asked: ‘Are they ungrateful to Allah Almighty?’ He صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم replied: ‘They are ungrateful to their husbands and the favours they receive from their husbands. So if you treat a woman well throughout your life, but she sees something that is to be disliked from you, she will say: I have never seen goodness from you .’

(Sahih Bukhari, Kitab-un-Nikah, vol. 3, pp. 463, Hadees 5197)

There is good news of Paradise for the Islamic sisters who wisely play the role of being obedient and grateful to their husbands. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has stated: ‘The woman who offers all five Salahs, observes Siyam of Ramadan, protects her honour and chastity and obeys her husband, it will be said to her: ‘Enter Paradise through whichever door of Paradise you wish.’

(Musnad Imam Ahmad, vol. 1, pp. 406, Hadees 1661)

Remember! A wife should also avoid being ungrateful to her husband because such type of thing if continues, it brings about hatred and hostility in the heart of her husband. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ forbid, if their relations break up due to this, there will be nothing left, but regret throughout her life. Anyhow, a successful wife is she who never utters words of ungratefulness and who always gives her husband pleasure by being grateful to him. This beautiful and lovely relationship of love and affection between wife and husband may continue in a proper manner only when they both fulfil the rights of each other in a right way.

صَلُّوۡا عَلَى الۡحَبِيۡب صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰى عَلٰى مُحَمَّد

Attributes of Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَلَيۡهِ السَّلَام

Dear Islamic sisters! Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has blessed Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام with countless attributes, and he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم has been mentioned at many places in the Holy Quran. He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام is the one who built Ka’bah Mu’azzamah and the

founder of Makkah tul Mukarramah. Sayyiduna Ibraheem عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام is ‘ جَدُّ الۡعَرب ’ (the grandfather of the Arab) and he [Sayyiduna Isma’eel] عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام is ‘ اَبُو الۡعَرب ’ (the father of the Arab). Aab-e-Zamzam is his Muajiza-e-Irhaasi [a miracle that he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم performed before the declaration of Nabuwat]. It will continue until the Judgement Day. Only two miracles will remain until the Judgement Day: (1) Spring of the Zamzam Water which has sprung out by virtue of the heel of Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام (2) The religion of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم i.e., the Holy Quran, blessed Hadees and their laws and acts of worship. (Tafseer-e-Na’eemi, vol. 16, pp. 285)

Remembrance of Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَلَيۡهِ السَّلَام

Dear Islamic sisters! The miracle of Zamzam Water that will continue to exist until the Judgement Day, is superior and preferable to all types of water in the world by virtue of its relation to Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم .

Many years have passed, but still this water is benefiting the creation of Allah Almighty and also distributing the blessings of the cure of apparent and spiritual diseases. (Al-Firdaus-ul-Akhbar, vol. 1, pp. 309, Hadees 2255)

-3 Zamzam water is better than every water [that is] present on the surface of the earth.

(Al-Mu’jam-ul-Kabeer, vol. 11, pp. 80, Hadees 11167)

Dear Islamic sisters! Have you heard how Allah Almighty has blessed this sacred water with great glory and blessings! In the Holy Quran, the blessed name of Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام has been mentioned at many places and the Zikr of his glory and eminence has been stated everywhere. At one place Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ has stated his (Sayyiduna Isma’eel’s عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام) attributes in the following words, in Ayah 54, 55 of Surah Maryam, Part 16:

وَ اذْكُرْ فِی الْكِتٰبِ اِسْمٰعِیْلَ٘-اِنَّهٗ كَانَ صَادِقَ الْوَعْدِ وَ كَانَ رَسُوْلًا نَّبِیًّاۚ(۵۴) وَ كَانَ یَاْمُرُ اَهْلَهٗ بِالصَّلٰوةِ وَ الزَّكٰوةِ۪-وَ كَانَ عِنْدَ رَبِّهٖ مَرْضِیًّا(۵۵)

Translation from Kanz-ul-Iman: And remember Isma’eel in the Book; he was indeed true to his promise and was a Noble Messenger, a Prophet. He used to command his people to offer prayer and give charity, and was liked by his Lord. (Part 16, Surah Maryam, Ayah 54-55)

Dear Islamic sisters! In the above-mentioned blessed Ayah, four great attributes of Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام have been mentioned: (1) He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام was true to his promise (2) He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام was the one who would give news of Ghayb (3) He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام was the one who would enjoin his family members to offer Salah and give Zakah (4) He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام was the favourite bondman of Allah Almighty.

True to promise!

Dear Islamic sisters! Here learn it by heart that all the blessed Ambiya عَـلَـيْـهِمُ الـصَّلٰوةُ وَالـسَّـلَام are true to promise, but Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام has an excellent reputation for this attribute. In his childhood, he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام fulfilled the promise very well that he made to his respected father, Sayyiduna Ibraheem Khaleelullah عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام with patience and pleasure at the time of being slaughtered. (Tafseer-e-Khaazin, vol. 4, pp. 22)

Once, he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم made a promise to a person that he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام would meet him at a place. He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام reached the place, but that person who was supposed to

come, forgot and even evening started. He عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام spent the whole night at that place. In the morning, when that person came he was astonished to see Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم there. He humbly said, ‘Did you not leave?’ Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام replied, ‘No, how could I leave before you came?’ (Tafseer Tabari, vol. 4, pp. 351)

Dear Islamic sisters! Have you heard how excellent attribute Sayyiduna Isma’eel عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام possessed! Despite even being a Nabi he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلاَم stayed the whole night at that place in order to fulfil the promise he عَـلَيْـهِ الـسَّـلَام made to one of his Ummatis [followers].

Therefore, if we do not have any Shari compulsion, so adopting this very beautiful habit we should definitely fulfil the promise we make to someone. Alas! Today breach of promise is becoming widespread in our society. It is not regarded as bad though breach of promise is Haraam and a grave sin because fulfilling a promise is Wajib and compulsory for a Muslim as per Shari’ah. In Ayah 1 of Surah Al-Ma`idah, Part 6, Allah Almighty has said:

Translation from Kanz-ul-Iman: O People who believe! Fulfil your words (agreements). (Part 6, Surah Al-Ma`idah, Ayah 1)

Similarly, in Ayah 34 of Surah Bani Israel, Part 15, Allah Almighty has stated:

اِنَّ الْعَهْدَ كَانَ مَسْـٴُـوْلًا (۳۴)

Translation from Kanz-ul-Iman: Indeed the promise will be asked about.

(Part 15, Surah Bani Israel, Ayah 34)

It is stated in another blessed Hadees: People will not be destroyed until they break agreement with their people. (Sunan Abi Dawood, vol. 4, pp. 166, Hadees 4347)

What is breach of promise?

Dear Islamic sisters! Have you heard how severe punishments there are for those who break promises! The one who breaks promises incurs the curse of Allah Almighty, His angels and all human beings. Neither any of her Fard act nor Nafl act is accepted. The Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم has said: Breach of promise does not mean that a person makes a promise [to someone] and he also intends to fulfil it, but rather breach of promise is that a person makes a promise, but he intends not to fulfil it. (Al-Jami’-ul-Akhlaq Ar-Rawi Al-Khateeb-ul-Baghdad, pp. 315, Hadees 1168)

In another blessed Hadees, it is stated: When a person makes a promise to his brother with the intention to fulfil it, but could not do so, and does not come at the appointed time, so there is no sin for him . (Sunan Abi Dawood, vol. 4, pp. 388, Hadees 4995)

Sawm of Arafah

Sayyiduna Abu Qatadah رَضِىَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهُ narrates the following blessed statement of the Holy Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم, ‘I have hope to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ that the Sawm of ‘Arafah (9 th Zil-Hajjah) removes the sins of the previous year and the next year.’ (Sahih Muslim, pp. 590, Hadees 196)

Sayyidatuna ‘Aaishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهَا has narrated that the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم said, ‘The Sawm of ‘Arafah (9th Zil-Hajjah-tul-Haraam) is equal to a thousand Siyam.’ (Shu’ab-ul-Iman, vol. 3, pp. 357, Hadees 3764)

Now not only hundreds of thousands of cassettes and VCDs of Sunnah-inspiring speeches and Madani Muzakarahs are reaching all over the world through Maktaba-tul-Madinah, but at the same time a large number of the Urdu translation of the books of Imam Ghazali رَحْمَةُ اللهِ تَعَالٰی عَلَيْه, the books of A’la Hadrat, the books of Ameer Ahl-e-Sunnat and Al-Madina-tul-‘Ilmiyyah are also being published and made public.

Madani pearls of treating relatives well!

At the end of the speech, let’s have the privilege to listen to a few Madani pearls of treating relatives well. First, listen to two blessed sayings of the Beloved Rasool صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالٰى عَلَيْهِ وَاٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّم:

… ٭ Every good treatment is Sadaqah whether it is with a rich person or a Faqeer. (Majma’-uz-Zawaid, vol. 3, pp. 331, Raqm 4754)

… ٭ The one who treats his parents well, congratulations to him because Allah Almighty has increased his age. (Al-Mustadrak, vol. 5, pp. 213, Hadees 7339)

… ٭ Treating relatives well is Wajib and breaking ties with them is Haraam and an act leading to Hell. (Bahar-e-Shari’at, vol. 3, pp. 558)

… ٭ Kind treatment does not only mean treating those relatives kindly who have treated you kindly. This would be actually an ‘exchange of treatment’. For example, sending a gift to the relative who has sent you a gift. In actual fact, kind treatment means establishing ties with one who cuts off ties with you and caring about the relative who ignores you and stays away from you. (Rad-dul-Muhtar, vol. 9, pp. 678)

… ٭ One can treat his relatives with kindness in different ways. For example, giving them gifts, helping them if they need it, saying Salam to them, meeting them, sitting with them, talking to them and treating them gracefully and nicely. (Kitab Ad-Durad, vol. 1, pp. 323)

… ٭ Not fulfilling need of relative is sin: When any of the relatives requests help for the fulfilment of her need we must help her because turning down this request is like breaking ties with her. (Kitab Ad-Durad, vol. 1, pp. 323)

For further details about treating relatives well, please study the booklet ‘ Hatho Hath Phuphi say Sulah Ker li ’, written by Shaykh-e-Tareeqat, Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat دَامَـتْ بَـرَكَـاتُـهُـمُ الْـعَـالِـيَـه .
